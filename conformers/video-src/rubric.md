# Scoring rubric — "Conformers" explainer video (each criterion 0–10; overall = unweighted mean; PASS = overall >= 8 AND no criterion < 6)

1. Content fidelity & depth — covers the six chapters of https://0x4d44.github.io/conformers/ (single-bond axles / σ vs π, conformation vs configuration, strain types, Newman projections & ethane, butane profile, barrier heights & rate, ring strain / Baeyer / combustion data, chair / axial-equatorial / ring flip, substituents / 1,3-diaxial / A-values, toolkit & quiz) with the real numbers, and is scientifically accurate. 
2. Narration quality — clear, well paced, sentence-level structure, appropriate length (~5–6 min), GLaDOS-flavoured voice used with taste; script reads well and teaches.
3. Visual style fidelity — matches the brief: gouache via p5.js + p5.brush: flat opaque fills in few layers, no transparent washes, dry-brush/charcoal edges, slightly chalky muted palette, paper-grain overlay, same seed every frame (no flicker/boil).
4. Explanatory clarity of visuals — each scene's imagery actually illustrates what is being said at that moment; labels legible; no overlapping/clipped text; nothing hidden behind the caption bar; consistent colour semantics.
5. Animation & sync — motion is smooth enough, purposeful, and timed to the narration cues; scene transitions; no dead/empty screens.
6. Music — original composition, appropriate mood, supports without masking speech, evolves over the piece.
7. Sound effects — well chosen, synced to on-screen events, Portal-esque flavour (PA chime, portal whooshes, turret pings/servos, buzzer etc.), mixed at sensible level.
8. Easter eggs & charm — Portal cake gags, turret cameo, the "deadlock resolution / interpreting unclear answer as" button; fun without derailing the teaching.
9. Technical quality — 1280x720 H.264+AAC, loudness/clipping, A/V sync, file size sane, nothing broken.
10. Overall polish & cohesion — feels like one finished piece worth publishing on the site.
