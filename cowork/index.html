The spectrum

In the loop, on the loop, off the loop

Three surfaces, one account, the same frontier models. Pick by how much of the work you want to hold in your own hands.

STATION 01

Claude.ai

Chat · in the loop

A conversation. You ask, Claude answers, you steer the next turn. Best for thinking out loud, drafting, research, and getting your head around a problem before anyone writes a line.

  • Artifacts you can edit live, Projects that hold your context
  • Connectors reach your docs, calendar and tools
  • Web, desktop and phone — wherever the idea strikes
You drive every turn. Nothing happens without you.
STATION 02

Claude Code

Build · on the loop

An agent that lives in your terminal and editor. It reads your files, runs commands, makes the change and shows its working — with you watching over its shoulder, approving as it goes.

  • Real edits to a real repo on your own machine
  • Runs tests, greps, git — the whole toolbelt
  • Hooks, subagents and skills to shape how it works
You supervise. It acts; you keep a hand on the wheel.
STATION 03

Claude Cowork

Delegate · off the loop

Hand off the whole task. Claude spins up its own sandboxed machine in the cloud, clones your repo fresh, works for as long as the job takes, and comes back with a branch, a pull request, or an answer.

  • Async: kick it off from your phone and walk away
  • Runs many jobs in parallel, each in its own box
  • Triggers on a schedule or a pull-request event
You delegate. It works while you do something else.
The new thing · Cowork

Delegation, not supervision

Chat and Code both keep you present. Cowork is the first surface built for the work you don't want to babysit — the long tail of chores, fixes and investigations that never quite reach the top of your day.

Its own clean room

Each job runs in an isolated, ephemeral cloud container — the repo cloned fresh, nothing shared with your laptop. Reclaimed when it's done. Nothing you didn't commit sticks around.

Many hands at once

Dispatch a dozen tasks and they run side by side, each in its own box. The refactor, the flaky test, the doc sweep and the dependency bump don't have to queue behind each other — or behind you.

On a schedule

Routines wake a session on a cron you set — an hourly triage, a nightly changelog, a Monday-morning digest. The work shows up finished, the way a report lands in your inbox.

Reacts to your repo

Subscribe a session to a pull request and it watches CI and review comments — re-running, diagnosing and pushing fixes until the checks go green, then telling you when they do.

Leaves a trail

You get a branch and a pull request, not a mystery. The diff is the record of what changed; the session is the record of why. Review it like any colleague's work — because that's what it is.

Meets you where you are

Start a job from the web, your phone, the desktop app or a GitHub Action. Close the tab. The container keeps working, and pings you when there's something to look at.

The handoff

One thought, passed down the line

You don't choose one surface for good. A single piece of work slides along the dial as it firms up — and because it's the same account and the same models, the context comes with it.

The dial isn't a menu you pick from once. It's a gearbox — you shift up as the work gets clearer and you need your hands less.
