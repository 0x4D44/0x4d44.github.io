A Standard for the Transmission of Scrolls on Avian Carriers, from Colinton to Glasgow

Run a pigeon post from Colinton to Glasgow. Work out, without being told, how many birds the sky will bear.

Status of this Memo, and how to play (20 seconds, 2 pigeons)

Status of this Memo

This memo provides information for the pigeon community. It does not specify a standard of any kind. Distribution is unlimited, as is, apparently, the number of ways a bird can go missing. Request for Comments: please do not send comments by pigeon.

How to play (20 seconds, 2 pigeons)

  1. Choose a number of birds and press Release the flock. Every flight is one round.
  2. Each bird carries one numbered scroll. Birds that arrive send back an ACK: a smaller bird with a smaller scroll. Lost scrolls are flown again, first.
  3. The Kilsyth Gap can only take so many birds at once, and its limit is not posted anywhere: you must work it out from what arrives. Too few birds and you miss the deadline; too many and the sky goes badly wrong.
  4. You learn of losses only after each flight, and never why. Like a real sender. Like a real, slightly worried sender.

    How to play, again, slower

    Each round you choose how many birds to send. Each bird carries one scroll. Birds that arrive send an ACK back; birds that do not are not coming back.

    The Gap has a limit you cannot see, and it may move. Fly too few and you miss the deadline. Fly too many and the Gap jams: in a crowd the scrum takes out more birds than the ones that did not fit, so offering more can deliver less.

    Some birds may be lost for reasons that have nothing to do with crowding. Learn to tell which losses are about you. Nobody can do it perfectly.

    Stars: one for delivering in time, two for doing it respectably, three for doing it quickly and tidily (the numbers are on each level's brief). The Reno, an autopilot you unlock after level 1, halves his flock at every sign of loss, which is sensible and also sometimes ridiculous. Stars do not count while he flies.

    Pigeon Protocol is a game about TCP congestion control. No birds were harmed; several were written about.
