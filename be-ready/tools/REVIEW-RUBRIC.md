# Be Ready - review rubric

Each reviewer scores only the criteria in their brief, 0-10, with concrete evidence for every point deducted.
A 9+ has to be earned: list what you checked, and every defect you found, with file/line or timestamp.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 10 means |
|---|-----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Source fidelity & factual accuracy | 20% | Every number/claim traces to the cited official page or is clearly labelled as non-official; nothing misquoted |
| 2 | Safety correctness (stove, CO, generator, water treatment, fuel storage) | 15% | No advice that could plausibly hurt someone; risks stated plainly; caveats where installers/manuals rule |
| 3 | Completeness vs brief | 15% | Gov list + stretch list; water amount; purification; electricity; cooking; stove heating AND cooking; food; comms; people/pets/baby |
| 4 | Actionability & interactivity | 15% | Tickable persistent lists, household sizing, calculators, a stove check, a what-to-do-when timeline; all of it works |
| 5 | Design, responsiveness, accessibility | 10% | No horizontal scroll at 390/768, nothing under the Almanac pill, keyboard + screen-reader sound, light/dark, print |
| 6 | Video (about 5 min, sound + audio) | 15% | Clear narration, sync'd captions, accurate visuals, no overlap/cut-off, balanced audio, ~5:00, plays in site |
| 7 | Integration & conventions | 5% | data.js entry, shelf, tag, back-pill include, repo tests pass, nothing stray committed |
| 8 | Honesty about limits | 5% | Unofficial status, uncertainty and non-UK-source figures flagged |

Pass: weighted mean > 9.0, no criterion < 8.0, and no open safety-critical defect.
