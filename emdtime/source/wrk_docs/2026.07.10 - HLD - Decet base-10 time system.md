# HLD — Decet: a base-10 timekeeping system on a tuned Earth

Status: implemented (v0.1.0). Author: Claude (with a 4-philosophy design panel + solver verification).

## 1. Brief & priorities

Design a new time system whose **top priority is mathematical efficiency in base-10**,
with divisions that mirror minute/hour/day for everyday use (math convenience breaks ties).

Fixed vs. tunable:
- **Second** — stays the SI second (Cesium-133; may adopt an ≥-accurate optical-lattice
  clock of the *same length*). Never changes.
- **Day** — one Earth rotation. Rotation rate is tunable, but equatorial surface speed must
  be **> 1 km/s and < 1% c**.
- **Year** — one revolution. Orbit is tunable, but must stay in the **habitable zone** and
  orbital speed **< 1% c**.

## 2. The two binding constraints (verified: `tools/solver.py`, `src/core` tests)

1. **The day must shrink.** Earth's present equatorial speed is 0.464 km/s — it *fails* the
   >1 km/s floor. So the day must be **< 40,075 s (≤ 11.13 conventional hours)**. The brief
   forces a spin-up; this is not a design choice.
2. **Day and year cannot both be pure powers of ten in the HZ.** A year of 10^k seconds lands
   the orbit at 0.465 AU (Venus-hot) or 2.16 AU (Mars-cold). So exactly one small integer
   factor in the year is unavoidable.

Both presets tune the orbit so the year is an **exact whole number of days ⇒ no leap years**.

## 3. Decision: two models, one orbit

Both sit at the same tuned orbit **a ≈ 1.171 AU** (year = 4.0×10⁷ s, conservative HZ,
insolation 0.729). They differ only in spin:

| | **Decet Standard** (flagship, default) | **Decet Terra** (gentle variant) |
|---|---|---|
| Day | **10,000 s** (10⁴, "one myriad") | 40,000 s (4×10⁴) |
| Year | **4000 days** = 4 seasons | 1000 days (10³) |
| Equatorial speed | 4.008 km/s | 1.002 km/s |
| Equatorial gravity | **74%** (26% lighter) | 98.4% (near-normal) |
| Day length | ~2.78 conv h | ~11.11 conv h |
| Clock | pure 4-digit second-counter `.SSSS` | `.####` with a 4-second finest grain |

**Why Standard is the default.** The clock is the most-used surface. Only a 10⁴-s day makes
time-of-day a pure 4-digit decimal counter (0000–9999) whose *every prefix is the fraction of
the day* (`.5000` = midday) — the brief's flagged prize, and it exists at no other day length.
The brief states math convenience wins ties, and the planetary costs (lighter equator, short
day, colder climate) are a *sunk consequence of the locked constraints* (the planet is being
re-engineered regardless), not a fresh cost this choice introduces. The one physics-forced
factor of 4 is spent on the year→season boundary, where four real seasons make it a feature and
it never touches the clock. **Terra** is offered because the 26% gravity drop and 2.78-h day are
severe; Terra keeps a near-normal planet at the cost of a messier clock. The app ships both and
lets the user explore the trade-off live (Planet tab).

## 4. Units

Sub-day ladder (Standard) — pure powers of ten of the second, dual-named:

| Unit | Sym | Seconds | SI synonym | Feels like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| second | s | 1 | — | the atomic tick |
| milliday | md | 10 | dekasecond | a beat |
| centiday | cd | 100 | hectosecond | the "minute" (~1.7 min) |
| deciday | dd | 1000 | kilosecond | the "hour" (~16.7 min) |
| day | D | 10,000 | — | one rotation (one myriad s) |
| week | wk | 100,000 | — | 10 days |
| month | mo | 1,000,000 | — | 100 days = 10 weeks |
| season | se | 10,000,000 | — | 1000 days = 10 months |
| year | yr | 40,000,000 | — | 4000 days = 4 seasons |

## 5. Calendar

`year = 4 seasons × 10 months × 10 weeks × 10 days` (4×10×10×10 = 4000). The day-of-year is a
4-digit positional address **SMWD** that digit-slices exactly: thousands = season (0–3), hundreds
= month, tens = week, units = day. Only the season digit is sub-decade (the lone echo of the ×4).
Equinoxes/solstices are pinned to the clean season boundaries (days 0/1000/2000/3000).

## 6. Clock

Time-of-day = second-of-day counter, written `.SSSS` (day fraction). `.0000` midnight,
`.2500` quarter, `.5000` midday, `.7500` three-quarters. Watch form `AB:CD` reads like legacy
mm:ss (midday = 50:00). Full civil timestamp (ISO-8601 analog): `Yyyyy-Ddddd-Tssss`.

## 7. Under the hood — epoch & interop

- **Machine instant** = one integer of SI seconds since the epoch (**2000-01-01T00:00:00 UTC**),
  like Unix time but decimal-decomposable. Slice its digits → year / day-of-year / second-of-day.
- **Interop is at the instant level, never the calendar level** (a Decet year ≠ a Gregorian year).
  Both systems share the SI second + epoch, so an instant converts exactly both ways. Leap seconds
  are treated as a display-boundary concern (continuous atomic timeline internally).

## 8. Architecture (the app)

- `src/core/` — pure, no UI deps, fully tested (the correctness surface):
  - `constants.ts` physical constants · `physics.ts` Kepler/rotation/HZ + `evaluateModel`
  - `system.ts` `SystemConfig` + `DECET_STANDARD`/`DECET_TERRA` presets
  - `reckon.ts` instant ↔ calendar/clock decomposition + formatting
  - `convert.ts` conventional interop + duration helpers
- `src/ui/` — React (Vite): Clock (live dial), Convert, Calendar, Planet (tuner), Design.
- Config-driven: a model is a `SystemConfig`, so the day-length fork is a preset, not a fork in
  the code, and the tuner explores the brief's actual degrees of freedom.

## 9. Honest consequences (not hidden)

Lighter equator (74% g, Standard), short day/night decoupled from sleep (Standard), ~27% less
sunlight (colder; needs greenhouse forcing), oceans/atmosphere redistributed toward the equator.
The **second is untouched** — only the counting layers above it are decimal.

## 10. Open questions (from the design panel; not blocking)

- Fix the *exact* orbit radius (a ≈ 1.17121 AU) so the year is precisely 4000 days, not the
  rounded 1.171. (`evaluateModel` derives the precise value; the config label is nominal.)
- Formalize an official multi-rotation "long-day" super-unit for sleep, or leave it to culture?
- Climate remediation budget for insolation 0.729; confirm orbit circularization (e≈0).
- Keep vestigial month/week names for legal/interop text, or drop them for pure digit addressing?
- Engineering feasibility & blast radius of the spin-up itself (out of software scope).
