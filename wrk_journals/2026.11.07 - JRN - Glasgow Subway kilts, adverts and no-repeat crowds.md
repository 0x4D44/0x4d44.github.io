## How the kilt got there (variants measured with people_holes, four kilted people, full / LOD)
committed 119.5 / 62.0; C (adult flare, LOD ease) 87.8 / 67.1; D (+ follow the body's outline) 88.2 / 57.4 but bulged at the hands;
E (hands not measured) 94.7 / 56.3; G (rings never tuck in) 91.7 / 61.0; H (clear the span above) 85.1 / 45.6; I (convex hull) 80.2 / 56.7;
K (+1.5 cm front room for hip flexion) 63.3 / 47.1; final (sporran on the kilt) 60.8 / 42.8. Renders were the deciding check at every step:
the measure counts a hand within 3 cm of the cloth as a hole, so it can disagree with what looks right.
