## Facts to keep
- TD area is `Y9`. Platform berth `1000+N` = platform N (0-10). Partner berths `B00N`.
- 176 berths seen in 104 min; 110 in SMART; 66 unplaced. `****` is not a train.
- There is NO platform 11 today: a 2021 remodel took the old platform 10 out of use and
  relabelled platform 11 as 10 (Wikipedia "London King's Cross railway station"; OSM and SMART
  both stop at 10). The first design and the frozen rubric (axis 1) said "add 11 by hand";
  that was wrong and the model draws 0-10 only. OPEN for Arthur: amend the rubric text.
- Published figures used (all cited in `core/station.ts`): shed arches 105 ft wide, 800 ft
  long, 71 ft to the crown (Network Rail history page, Victorian Web); clock tower 112 ft
  (Grace's Guide; one page says 120 ft); Western Concourse apex 20 m, rim 5 m, radius 74 m
  (Architect magazine, Building, Proquest). Platform lengths 305/304/276 m for 0/1/10 come from
  a single blog: weak, shown only as context in `tools/fidelity.ts`.
- The extract also holds St Pancras and Underground platforms: use zone `KINGS_CROSS`.
- Recording: `~/kx-data/recordings/td-Y9-20261107.ndjson` (not in git). SMART/CORPUS in
  `~/kx-data/*.json.gz` (not in git; licence unchecked).
