# Daily Flange motorsport desk

## Goal

Add a Motorsport child desk under Sport with 50 short, fact-based articles about
strange real motorsport incidents. Every article needs its own editorial image.
Arthur has already authorised integration after the finished exact-tree gate.

## Decisions

- Keep the existing two-level Daily Flange taxonomy: Sport remains the parent
  and Motorsport becomes a leaf beside Football, Cricket, Olympics, Tennis,
  Athletics, and Other Sports.
- Use mot- article IDs and matching news/images/mot-*.webp assets.
- Keep each factual piece compact: enough context to explain the incident without
  turning the 50-story desk into 50 long reads.
- Avoid selecting incidents whose only noteworthy feature is a fatality.
