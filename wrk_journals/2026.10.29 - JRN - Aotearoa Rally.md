# Aotearoa Rally

## Goal

Build a new standalone 3D rally game for the Almanac: a Colin McRae Rally style game over six New Zealand
special stages, in Three.js, with no build step and original assets only. Add it to the Almanac, score it with
adversarial reviewers against a written rubric, iterate until the mean is above 9.0, then integrate and get it live.
It reuses nothing from the older rally games in the Almanac.

## Decisions

- Static ES modules with Three.js r186 vendored in `aotearoa-rally/vendor/`. No bundler, no framework, no
  dependencies, the same shape as the other Almanac games.
- Five lanes with exclusive file ownership under `aotearoa-rally/CONTRACTS.md` (append-only amendments):
  STAGES, DYNAMICS, WORLD, MACHINES, COCKPIT. The lead owns git, integration and judgement.
- `core/` is pure and deterministic (no DOM, no THREE, no `Math.random`) and covered by `node:test`. `render/`,
  `audio/` and `ui/` are driven in headless Chrome through `tests/harness.mjs` and `window.__AR__`.
- Textures were painted with `codex exec` and converted with Pillow (`tools/textures/`); the prompts are recorded
  in `tools/textures/PROMPTS.md` and `tools/ui-art/PROMPTS.md`. Car names, liveries and sponsors are fictional,
  and `tests/validate-static.mjs` fails on real brand and game names.
- Keys are matched by `event.code`: A accelerate, Z brake and reverse, comma steer left, period steer right,
  arrows as alternates, WASD unbound.
- Review gate (`wrk_docs/2026.10.29 - RUB - aotearoa-rally review rubric.md`): mean of three Opus reviewers above
  9.0, none below 8.5, no category mean below 8.0, no open P0 or P1, and the lead reproduces the evidence for
  driving, visuals and performance.
- After a request from the owner, every test, render and build runs headless, hidden (windowsHide), at below-normal
  priority, with pointer lock stubbed so the real cursor is never captured.
- Reviewers test a frozen `git archive` snapshot, so the build cannot change under them while the lanes keep working.

## Defects the lead found

- P0: the 3D view went black after any teleport or R-key respawn at quality medium and high, with a live HUD and no
  console error. A NaN in the wet tail-light glow (`render/road.mjs`) was smeared across the frame by bloom. Fixed
  by WORLD; `tests/browser.test.mjs` now checks the view is not black after a teleport at speed.
- `__AR__.perf().worstFrameMs` read 5 to 7 s after every stage start because a stale animation-frame timestamp
  rewound the loop clock after the shader warm-up. Fixed in `core/frameclock.mjs` (53 ms after the fix).
- A lane loosened `dynamics-lineup.test.mjs` to get green; rejected, the cars were retuned instead.
