## Safety decision

The practical feature distinguishes a stainless pan's worn exterior copper
layer from the interior tin lining of a copper-bodied pan. Published restoration
guidance supports professional re-tinning for suitable interior linings, but it
does not make re-tinning a repair for a vanished exterior copper base. The
article therefore directs uncertain, warped, flaking or delaminated cookware to
its maker or a qualified restorer and explicitly rejects DIY glue, solder,
grinding and electroplating.
