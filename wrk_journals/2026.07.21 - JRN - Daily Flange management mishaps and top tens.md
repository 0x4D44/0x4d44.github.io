# Daily Flange management mishaps and top tens

## Goal

Add 50 fictional Darwin-Award-inspired reports satirising middle management,
plus ten complete preposterous top-ten articles. Keep the humour aimed at
corporate incentives and self-inflicted career damage, without using real
victims, firms, deaths or copied award anecdotes.

## Decisions

- Make `Middle Management` a child desk of `Business`; `Business` remains a
  genuine leaf so its existing 66 stories stay reachable under “All Business”.
- Prefix the mishap articles and images with `mgmt-`; prefix the list articles
  and images with `top10-`.
- Give every article unique AI-generated editorial artwork and accessible alt
  text. Keep the scenes non-graphic and avoid logos or embedded text.
- Structure each mishap as four paragraphs: policy, escalation, consequence,
  then the management response. Structure each ranking as an introduction,
  exactly ten descending entries, and a conclusion.
- Label the mishaps as fictional career-selection satire. The joke removes the
  manager from the succession plan, not a person from the gene pool.

## Image generation record

Mode: built-in image generation (`imagegen` skill).

Shared prompt treatment: a wide 16:9 Daily Flange newspaper hero, polished
editorial illustration, realistic proportions, dry British satire and a subtle
newspaper-print texture. Every scene explicitly excluded injuries, gore, real
logos, trademarks, watermarks and readable text. The subject-specific portion
described the fictional mishap or ranked objects in the matching article.

The source PNGs were centre-cropped to 16:9 and installed as 1600×900 WebP at
quality 82. One hot-desk poster and one embossed biscuit were precisely edited
to remove accidental readable lettering without changing their compositions.

Final saved paths are the 50 one-to-one management assets at
`news/images/mgmt-<article-id-suffix>.webp` and the ten one-to-one ranking
assets at `news/images/top10-<article-id-suffix>.webp`. The focused validator
checks every referenced path, enforces uniqueness within both sets and confirms
that every file exists.
