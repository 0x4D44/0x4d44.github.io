# Daily Flange almanac adverts and review season

## Goal

Advertise every Almanac document added since the last advert sweep in The Daily
Flange, and review all of them — not pooled on one page, but spread across the
paper, including a new Reviews desk under Life.

## Scope: what counts as "new"

The hand-written cross-promos in `news/ads.js` stopped at documents dated
2026-07-17 (`costa-concordia` was the last). Everything dated 2026-07-18 or
later had been falling through to the generated fallback campaign at the bottom
of that file, which uses the catalog tagline verbatim.

That gives a clean cut of **19 documents**: `great-dying`, `world-in-640k`,
`animal-defenses`, `master-flanger`, `win2k`, `quantum-theory`, `thermal-mind`,
`black-holes`, `teenage-brain`, `road-wear`, `root-and-crown`, `african-star`,
`iron-vertex`, `lost-valley-dinosaurs`, `game-of-dracula`, `instruments`,
`arran-deep-time`, `stratego`, `influence`. (`news` itself is excluded — the
paper does not advertise itself to itself.)

## Decisions

- **Adverts: vector, not raster.** The 18 existing image-backed campaigns use
  `images/ad-<slug>.webp` editorial art. The 19 new ones use the `icon` path
  into `window.AD_ICONS` instead, so the whole intake ships without adding
  ~19 MB of generated art. Seventeen new badges were drawn for it.
- **Icon overlays must be dark, not white.** `currentColor` in an ad badge is
  the ad's `fg`, which is `#fff` on every one of these campaigns — so detail
  drawn as `#fff` at low opacity is invisible. The first pass rendered `floppy`
  as a plain white square, `piano` as a white slab and `brain` as a shrub.
  Overlay detail is now `#000` at 0.28–0.55, matching the existing `chip` badge.
- **Reviews is a leaf under Life**, alongside Lifestyle/Weather/Horoscopes/
  Obituaries, with its own `CAT` colour and a new `rosette` article glyph.
- **The scorecard renders from a field, not a category.** An article's optional
  `review: { subject, stars, verdict, href, linkText }` renders the star box, so
  the Science, Health, Technology, Engineering, Lifestyle and Voices reviews are
  scored exactly like the ones on the Reviews desk. Stars are 0–5 in halves; the
  half glyph is a `background-clip: text` split with an `@supports` fallback.
- **A review needed its own disclaimer.** The default footer says the story
  "never happened" and that "nothing here is true", which is wrong for a column
  about a document the reader can click through to. `footerHtml` gained a
  `"review"` mode saying the reviewed document is real and the reviewer is not;
  each review also carries a `Review notice` in the body disclaimer. The old
  truthy call signature (`footerHtml(basedOnTruth)`) still works.
- **Spread, not a pile.** 8 on Reviews, 3 Science, 2 Technology, 2 Health,
  2 Voices, 1 Engineering, 1 Lifestyle, plus a Letters postbag replying to the
  season. The desk was chosen by subject: teeth to Health, potholes to
  Engineering, the bombardier beetle to an Opinion column.
- **Left `Sparks fly` alone.** It carries an exact count assertion (201) as a
  series guard; adding a review there would have meant editing that guard for an
  unrelated reason. Voices and Lifestyle took the opinion-side reviews instead.

## Validation record

- `node news/tests/validate-static.mjs` passes at 1,124 articles (was 1,104:
  19 reviews + 1 letters page).
- New coverage in that validator: exactly 19 scorecards, no document reviewed
  twice, the desk spread asserted exactly, every `review.href` matching
  `../<slug>/` **and existing on disk**, stars in whole/half increments within
  0–5, every review carrying a `Review notice` that says the document is real,
  and every reviewed slug having a hand-written advert above the generated
  fallback (checked against the file text before the fallback block, so a
  generated ad cannot satisfy it).
- Render coverage: each review renders its scorecard with exactly five star
  glyphs and an `aria-label` ending "out of five", links out to its document,
  omits "never happened" / "Nothing here is true", and carries the review
  footer. A 4.5-star review renders exactly one half glyph; a 5-star one renders
  none.
- `npm run build` (all static validators) passes.
- `news/tests/browser.test.mjs` passes with the widened Life dropdown.
- `npm run test:responsive` passes — no document scrolls sideways.
- Measured `documentElement.scrollWidth` at a true 390×844 mobile viewport on
  four review pages and two pre-existing articles: 390 on all six, no overflow.
  (A `--window-size` screenshot without `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride`
  clips the render and looks like overflow. It is not.)
- Visually reviewed: a contact sheet of all 17 new ad badges on an ad gradient
  (twice — the first pass is described above), a full review article at 1280px,
  the Reviews desk listing, and the scorecard at 390px including the half star.

## Catalog copy

`data.js` said the paper had "790 deadpan articles". It has had more than that
for some time; the news validator's own lockstep assertion was at 1,104. Updated
to 1,124 and the Reviews desk mentioned.

## Notes for next time

- The generated fallback at the bottom of `news/ads.js` means a new document is
  never *absent* from the ad rotation, only unremarkable. Finding the real gap
  means diffing `window.ESSAYS` against the explicit `href: "../<slug>/"` list
  above the fallback block — not against the rendered ads.
- Chrome refuses to start as root without `--no-sandbox`, and neither
  `news/tests/browser.test.mjs` nor `tests/responsive.test.mjs` passes that
  flag. A one-line wrapper script in `CHROME_PATH` that execs
  `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium --no-sandbox "$@"` is enough to run both.
