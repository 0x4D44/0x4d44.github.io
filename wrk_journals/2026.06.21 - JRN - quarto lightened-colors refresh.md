# 2026.06.21 — JRN — Quarto lightened-colors refresh

Applied a revised Quarto build from `~/Downloads/Quarto (1).zip` (a visual
refresh of the [[2026.06.20 import]]).

## What actually changed
Diffed the new zip against the live slug. The **only** genuine upstream change
is `quarto-themes.jsx`: the `pieceDark` swatch in all three themes (Salon,
Atelier, Tabletop) is lightened from near-black to a warmer mid-brown with a
brighter sheen, so the dark pieces read clearly instead of as black blobs.
3 lines, +6 bytes.

The new zip's `manifest.json`/`sw.js` are byte-identical to the v1 originals
(`start_url: ./play.html`, shell `./play.html`), i.e. they do **not** carry the
`play.html`→`index.html` promotion rewiring we applied on import. So I kept our
rewired live copies and did **not** overwrite them — only edited `sw.js` to bump
the cache version (below). `play.html` itself is unchanged from v1 (== our
`index.html`); all other jsx, the icons, and the catalog entry are untouched.

## Why bump the service-worker VERSION
`sw.js` caches `.jsx` **cache-first** under `VERSION`. A returning visitor who
already has the SW registered would keep the *old* `quarto-themes.jsx` (old
colors) forever, because the browser only re-installs the SW when `sw.js` itself
changes byte-wise. Bumping `quarto-v1` → `quarto-v2` makes the browser pick up
the new SW, whose `activate` handler deletes the old-version cache, so the stale
`quarto-themes.jsx` is purged and the next fetch gets the lightened colors. This
is what makes the refresh actually reach installed users — not scope creep.

## Catalog entry
Left `data.js` unchanged — same document, same tagline/icon/date. A colour tweak
doesn't warrant resurfacing it to the top via a date bump.

## Verified
Served the worktree over HTTP, confirmed the new `#6f4d37`… swatches and
`VERSION = 'quarto-v2'` are served, and headless-rendered `/quarto/`: the
onboarding pieces now show the dark pieces as a lighter brown (vs near-black in
the v1 render). Light pieces and layout unchanged.

## Re-import note
Same caveat as the import: a future re-import from a Downloads zip must re-apply
the `play.html`→`index.html` promotion + manifest/sw path rewiring, and (when
shipping an asset change behind the service worker) bump the `sw.js` VERSION.
