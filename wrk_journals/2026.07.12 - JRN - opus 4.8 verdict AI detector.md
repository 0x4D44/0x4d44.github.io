### Pass 1 results — engine hardening (done)
Built an offline validation harness (scratchpad, not shipped — repo has no test
suite by design) over a labelled corpus: 6 human (Dickens, Austen, Hemingway,
casual anecdote, terse tech note, Reddit-style), 5 AI (stock essay, listicle,
chatbot how-to, LinkedIn post, explainer), 2 mixed; plus 4 adversarial-human
cases (corporate-speak, formal memo, stray-buzzword).

**Finding:** initial engine had perfect precision (humans 0–13) but weak recall —
genuinely-AI paragraphs with varied rhythm slipped into "uncertain"/"human"
(one at 26). Every AI sample maxed `vocab`; the weighted average diluted it.

**Decision — corroborated escalation** (not a raw vocab floor). First tried a
vocab-only floor: lifted recall but FLAGGED a human corporate-speak message and a
formal human memo as "Likely AI" — unacceptable (false positives are the costly
error and the tool's whole pitch). Retuned so:
  - stock vocabulary ALONE only lifts to the "uncertain" band (≤48);
  - "Likely AI" requires vocab AND a second sustained signal (transition / triad /
    formulaic-opener / em-dash) — `min(vocab, max(those))*74`.
Also softened the vocab curve (÷14 → ÷18) and gated on `W≥40 && tellsAbs≥3` so a
lone stray buzzword can't move a short note. Escalation only ever LIFTS, so clean
human text (vocab≈0) is untouched.

**Outcome:** clean humans 0–13 (unchanged); corroborated AI → 74 "Likely AI";
vocab-only-AI and ambiguous-human both → 48 "Uncertain" (the honest answer).
Precision preserved, recall materially up. Verified via harness + adversarial set.

**Deferred (logged, not done):** a dedicated n-gram-repetition signal — the MATTR
`rep` signal already occupies that space and fired ~0 on the corpus; a new signal
was not justified by the data. Reversible to revisit.
