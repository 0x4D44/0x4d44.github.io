    How to command the field

    ObjectiveMovementCombatSpecialistsHistory
    01

    Capture the Flag

    Each commander secretly arranges forty pieces in the four rows nearest them. Red moves first. Capture the opposing Flag, or leave the enemy with no legal move, to win.

    02

    Move under cover

    Move one piece horizontally or vertically. No diagonals, jumping, sharing squares or entering either lake. Bombs and Flags never move. Scouts alone may travel any clear distance in one straight line.

    03

    Reveal only in battle

    When a piece attacks, both ranks are exposed. The stronger remains; the weaker is captured. Equal ranks are both removed unless the aggressor variation is selected.

    04

    Use the specialists

    Miner 8 is the only rank that can remove a Bomb.

    Spy S defeats the Marshal 1 only when the Spy attacks first.

    Scout 9 crosses any number of clear squares.

    05

    Do not shuttle forever

    A piece may cross the same two squares on three consecutive moves by that side; the fourth crossing is barred. Moving another piece breaks the sequence.

    06

    A Dutch modern classic

    Jacques Johan Mogendorff created the game before the Stratego name was registered in 1942. Post-war Dutch editions established the red and blue armies; Milton Bradley introduced the American edition in 1961. Different eras printed the Marshal as either 1 or 10; this edition prints the classic marking by default — 1 for the Marshal down to 9 for the Scout, with S, B and F for the Spy, Bombs and Flag — and offers the modern display as an option, without changing the rules.

    This adaptation follows the full modern 40-piece game and uses original code and artwork rather than scans or production assets.
