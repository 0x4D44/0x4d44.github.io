# HLD — Span of Control

A tongue-in-cheek Reigns-style decision game about middle management: too senior
for the coal-face, too junior for the direction, measured only by the size of
your team. Ships as a self-contained static document at `span-of-control/`.

## Design thesis

Middle management has **no direct verbs**. There is no "do work" button and no
"set strategy" button. Everything is routing, translation, and spin:

- Decisions arrive from above as fait accompli; your only lever is how you
  cascade them down.
- Work happens below you whether you help or not; your only lever is how you
  report it up.
- **Headcount is the score.** Your job title is a pure function of team size —
  nothing else — and is recalculated live. Lose people, get silently demoted.
- The central trap: everything you do to grow the team makes you a bigger
  reorg target. Empire-building is both the win condition and the death
  condition.
- Occasionally the game offers you the chance to do real work. Taking it is
  always a mistake.

Tone: **affectionate wry with absurdist escalation.** The player-manager is a
decent person trapped in a system of absurd incentives; the satire targets the
system, never the player, and never the team. Early quarters are realistic;
by quarter 8 the player is defending headcount to a committee that may not
exist. Britishisms welcome (the employer is a plc).

## Core loop

A stream of cards. Each card is a piece of corporate ephemera (email, calendar
invite, chat ping, corridor Post-it, memo, all-hands slide). Two choices —
swipe left / right, tap, or arrow keys. Each choice nudges meters:

| Meter       | Range  | 0 =                        | 100 =                                   |
|-------------|--------|----------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| LEADERSHIP  | 0–100  | managed out                | promoted out of the game (a win that's a loss) |
| TEAM        | 0–100  | mass resignation           | your team worships you; Leadership finds the cult concerning |
| YOU         | 0–100  | burnout                    | visible work-life balance; this is noticed |
| HEADCOUNT   | 0–40+  | role eliminated            | (soft cap) reorg-target risk scales with size |

- One card ≈ one week; 12 weeks = a quarter; quarter boundaries fire a forced
  ritual card (perf review, budget review, planning, engagement survey).
- Title ladder from headcount: 0–2 "Team Lead" → 3–5 "Manager" → 6–9 "Senior
  Manager" → 10–14 "Group Manager" → 15–20 "Head of" → 21–29 "Senior Head of"
  → 30+ "Director (interim)". Suffix flavour grows with absurdity
  ("of Horizontal Enablement").
- Reorg roulette at each quarter boundary: probability grows with headcount.
  A reorg is an arc, not instant death — survive it and you may absorb a team.
- Endings are punchline documents (HR letter, press release, exit-interview
  form, leaving-card). Target run length: 25–60 cards.

## The Laminated Wisdom (collection meta)

Management homilies unlocked by specific choices, endings, and thresholds —
mix of real clichés and invented absurdities, each attributed to a fictional
thought leader ("— Barry Vantage, author of *The Courage to Delegate
Courage*"). Persisted in localStorage; browsable as laminated cards pinned to
a corkboard. Collecting them IS the absurdist long-game: wisdom accumulates,
the job never changes. Unlock moment: laminated card slides in mid-run.

## Recurring cast (writers: keep voices consistent)

- **Diane Vane** — VP of Platform Synergy. Your boss. Speaks entirely in deck
  titles and reheated aphorisms. Not evil; weather.
- **Greg** — your most senior engineer. Competent, weary, sees through
  everything, stays anyway. The conscience of the game.
- **Priya** — ambitious mid-level who wants your job (why?). Excellent, keen,
  slightly terrifying.
- **Colin Farrier** — peer manager, headcount rival, master of the land-grab
  framed as collaboration.
- **Saskia** — HR Business Partner. Weaponised cheerfulness; nothing she says
  is ever quite a sentence.
- **The Consultant** — from Bainbridge McKallister. Name never offered.
  Appears mid-game; the Transformation Programme follows.
- **Marcus** — the new grad. Asks innocent, devastating questions.
- **Janet from Finance** — controls the reqs. The only person with actual
  power. Speaks rarely; when she does, it's final.

## Architecture (house pattern, mirrors shipshape/humanity-retention)

```
span-of-control/
  index.html            shell; no runtime external URLs
  styles.css            corporate-ephemera design system (see below)
  content.js            ES module: CARDS, HOMILIES, ENDINGS, TITLES, CAST, RITUALS
  engine.js             pure deterministic logic (seeded mulberry32 RNG)
  storage.js            localStorage: collection, run stats, settings
  app.js                DOM/UI, swipe gestures, keyboard
  manifest.webmanifest  PWA, start_url "./", scope "./"
  sw.js                 cache-first, versioned name span-of-control-v1
  icons/icon.svg
  fonts/*.woff2         self-hosted (Public Sans, Courier Prime, Playfair Display italic)
  tests/engine.test.mjs     deterministic engine + Monte Carlo balance oracle
  tests/content.test.mjs    schema/volume/tone-shape assertions on content
  tests/validate-static.mjs house static gate (files exist, no external URLs, node --check)
```

- Engine is pure: `newGame(seed)`, `currentCard(state)`, `choose(state, side)`
  → next state; UI never mutates state directly. Deterministic under seed for
  tests.
- Card schema: `{ id, cast, kind, title?, text, condition?, weight?, once?,
  minQuarter?, maxQuarter?, requiresFlags?, forbidsFlags?, left: CHOICE,
  right: CHOICE }` where CHOICE = `{ label, effects: {leadership?, team?,
  you?, headcount?}, setFlags?, clearFlags?, followup?, homily?, quip? }`.
  `quip` is a one-line narrator aftermath shown after choosing.
- Arcs = flags + `followup` scheduling with delay; rituals forced at quarter
  boundaries.
- localStorage key: `0x4d44.spanofcontrol.v1`.

### Visual system — "fluorescent-lit office, 4:51pm on a Thursday"

Fake corporate intranet chrome for **Consolidated Progress plc**. Warm-paper
cards on an institutional putty/greige field; 1990s annual-report burgundy +
navy accents; laminate sheen on homily cards; KPI-gauge meters (coffee-cup
fill for YOU). Fonts: Public Sans (UI), Courier Prime (memos), Playfair
Display italic (homilies/motivational). Card drag with rotation à la Reigns;
affected-meter dots hint on drag. Mobile-first; keyboard accessible.

## Content targets (asserted by tests)

- ≥ 100 decision cards across categories: upward mgmt, downward mgmt,
  peers/turf, process & ritual, consultant/transformation arc, HR & people,
  late-game absurdism, do-the-work-yourself traps.
- ≥ 40 homilies; ≥ 15 endings; every ending reachable in principle
  (meter/threshold mapping covered by tests).
- Balance oracle: Monte Carlo over seeds × policies (random, greedy-safe)
  asserts median run length in 25–60 cards and no unreachable ending class.
