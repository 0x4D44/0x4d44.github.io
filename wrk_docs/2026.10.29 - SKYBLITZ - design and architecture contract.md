## 1. Vision and quality bar

It must feel like **flying an aircraft** (banking turns, energy exchange in dives and climbs, loops, barrel rolls, stalls, G-feel), not driving a car or piloting a spaceship — wrapped in a fast, colourful, friendly arcade race: 8 racers, 3 laps, weapons and power-ups, shortcuts, rubber-banded AI that is *intelligent* (racing lines, item use, dodging, shortcuts, personalities).

Pillars: (1) flight feel first; (2) spectacle (colour, light, speed, particles, post-FX); (3) readable chaos (you always know where to fly, what hit you, what you hold); (4) fairness and fun (close races, comebacks, no cheap deaths); (5) polish (every transition, sound, popup and menu is finished).

"AAA-quality" here means: a reviewer playing for 30 minutes finds no placeholder art, no broken state, no dead UI, no silent actions, stable 60 fps on a mid GPU, and thinks "this is a shipped game". The review rubric is `wrk_docs/2026.10.29 - SKYBLITZ - review rubric.md`; target score > 9.0 in every category. Read it: it is how your work will be judged.

Art direction: bright, saturated, stylised-realistic (Mario Kart 8 / Wipeout / Pilotwings 64 energy): glossy clear-coat paint, chunky silhouettes, strong rim light, big soft clouds, bloom on emissives, atmospheric fog tinted by the sky, long wing-tip vapour trails. Never flat-grey, never unlit. Original content only: fictional pilots/brands, no trademarks, no copied assets.

## 2. Content

**Aircraft (6)** — see `src/craft/roster.js` (ids: `falcon wasp manta bulldog comet sprite`; stats 1..10; 3 liveries each). Distinct silhouettes: jet, light canard, flying wing, heavy twin-prop, delta, sport biplane.
**Pilots (7 AI)** — `PILOTS` in roster.js, each with personality numbers (skill, aggression, itemSense, stunts, shortcuts, consistency).
**Courses (4)** — ids `sunrise` (tropical dawn, easy), `redrock` (canyon golden hour), `neon` (night city), `frost` (glacier twilight + aurora, hard). Each: 5.5–7 km lap, 3 laps, lap ≈ 55–85 s, at least one **loop**, one natural **arch/tunnel**, one **shortcut** (risky, ~4–8 s saving), banked sweepers, tight sections, hazards, item-orb clusters ×5 per lap, boost rings, and a launch platform start grid.
**Items (10)** — `missile triple mine turbo turbo3 shield emp storm smoke drone` (`src/race/balance.js`). One slot. Rubber-banded by position (last place gets stronger items; leader gets weak ones).
**Primary cannon** — `controls.fire`: rapid bolts with a heat meter; hits cause a short stagger (speed loss), enough hits cause a stall tumble.
**Modes** — Quick Race, Grand Prix (4 races, points 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1, standings), Time Trial (solo, best-lap tracking). Difficulty: easy / normal / hard / expert. Laps default 3.
**Stunts** charge a boost meter: loop, manual roll, barrel roll, inverted flight, near-miss, perfect ring pass, shortcut, drafting.
**Hits** never destroy: they cause a *tumble* (≈1.2–1.8 s of spin, big speed loss) then brief invulnerability. A **shield** absorbs one hit. A **barrel roll** gives ≈0.6 s of missile-evasion (Star Fox deflect).

Default controls (flight convention; setting `pitchMode: 'flight'|'arcade'`):
`W/↑` push = nose DOWN, `S/↓` pull = nose UP · `A/←` `D/→` bank · `Q`/`E` or double-tap `A`/`D` barrel roll · `Shift` boost · `Ctrl`/`X` airbrake · `Space` cannon · `F`/`Enter` use item (`B`+item = backwards) · `V` look back · `C` camera · `Esc`/`P` pause.
Gamepad: left stick = pitch/bank, RT boost, LT airbrake, LB/RB barrel roll, A item, X cannon, Y camera, R3 look back, Start pause. Touch: virtual stick + buttons.

## 3. Conventions (all modules)

* Plain ES modules, no build step, no CDN. `import * as THREE from 'three'`; addons `three/addons/...` (vendored in `skyblitz/vendor`, Three r186). Use the vendored addons you need; do not add new dependencies.
* **Axes and units**: Three.js right-handed, **+Y up**; an aircraft's local **forward is −Z**, right wing +X, up +Y. Metres, seconds, radians. `right = forward × up`. Quaternions for orientation.
* **Fixed timestep**: gameplay advances ONLY via `fixedUpdate(dt)` with `dt = SIM_DT = 1/60`. Presentation advances in `update(dt, game)` / `render(alpha, dt)` per rendered frame. Anything drawn must use the **interpolated** transform `racer.render.pos/quat` (set by `race.render`), never `racer.flight.pos` directly.
* **Randomness**: gameplay randomness ONLY through the seeded `game.rng` (fork with `.fork(salt)`); `Math.random()` is allowed only for purely visual effects. This keeps runs reproducible for tests.
* **No allocations in hot paths** (per-frame/per-step): reuse scratch Vector3/Quaternion; pool particles/projectiles.
* **Module hygiene**: pure-logic modules (flight, course maths, race, ai, items, music scheduling) must not touch `document`/`window`/WebGL at import time so they run under `node:test`. THREE math (Vector3, Quaternion, geometries) is fine in Node.
* **Quality**: obey `game.q` (see `src/config.js` QUALITY): `particles`, `scenery`, `trails`, `clouds`, `shadows`, `water`, `post`, `aa`, `farMul`. Budget at `high`: ≤ 400 draw calls, ≤ 1.5 M triangles, 60 fps on a mid-range GPU. Use `InstancedMesh`/merged geometry for repeated props. No per-frame geometry creation. Textures ≤ 2048².
* **Missing assets never crash**: `game.assets.tex(name)` returns a placeholder until the file arrives; audio/music failures degrade silently.
* **UI/DOM rules** (UI owner, but everyone must respect): the Almanac's shared "← Almanac" pill (`/almanac-back.js`) is pinned top-left at 0,0, 109×41. **No interactive element may overlap x<112px & y<44px.** The page must never scroll horizontally at 390×844 or 768×1024 (`tests/responsive.test.mjs` fails the whole repo otherwise). No external requests at runtime.
* Style: 2-space indent, single quotes, `const`/`let`, JSDoc-light comments explaining *why*; match the surrounding code. Keep files under ~900 lines (split modules).
* Naming of events/fields below is **frozen**: you may add, never rename or remove.

## 5. Shared data contracts

### 5.1 Controls (`src/controls.js`)
```
{ pitch: -1..1 (+ = nose UP), roll: -1..1 (+ = bank RIGHT), yaw: -1..1 (+ = nose right),
  boost: bool, airbrake: 0..1, barrel: -1|0|1 (edge, consumed by flight when it starts), fire: bool,
  item: bool (edge), itemBack: bool (hold), lookBack: bool }
```
`input.sample()` is called once per fixed step and returns edge-latched controls. AI fills the same struct.

### 5.2 FlightState (`createFlightState(spec)` in `src/flight/flight.js`) — field names frozen
`spec, params, pos, quat, vel (world m/s), speed, forward, up, right (derived each step), bank (+ right wing down), pitch (+ nose up), heading, angVel (local rad/s), gLoad, throttle (0..1 for display), airbrake (0..1),`
`boost {meter 0..100, timer s, power, active}, barrel {t, dir, cooldown} (t>0 while rolling; **t>0 = missile-evasive**), stall {active,t}, tumble {t,dir} (t>0 = spinning, uncontrollable),`
`events []` — flight pushes `{type:'stunt', kind:'loop'|'roll'|'barrel'|'inverted', reward}` / `{type:'stall'}` / `{type:'recover'}`; **the Race drains this array every step** and turns entries into game events (and `addBoostMeter(fs, reward)`).
API (names frozen): `FLIGHT, flightParams(spec), topSpeed(spec), createFlightState(spec), resetFlightState(fs,pos,quat,speed), stepFlight(fs,controls,dt,ctx), applyTumble(fs,seconds,dir), applyImpulse(fs,v), startBoost(fs,seconds,power), addBoostMeter(fs,amount)`. `ctx = { speedMul?, assist? ('standard'|'pro'), gravityScale? }`.

### 5.3 Course (`src/world/course.js`; stub shows every member) — members frozen
```
id, name, def, length (m, one lap of the MAIN path), laps, group (THREE.Group of all visuals), environment, minimap
frameAt(s, out) -> out{pos,tangent,up,right,halfWidth,halfHeight,bank,curvature}     // main path, s wraps
project(pos, hintS, out) -> out{s,lateral(+right),vertical(+up),dist,branch(-1 main | branch index)}   // hint = previous s; MUST handle loops/self-crossings/branches
collide(pos, radius, proj, out) -> bool  out{kind:'ground'|'hazard'|'wall',depth,normal,soft}          // 'wall' = soft corridor edge
groundHeight(x,z) -> y          // terrain/water height under (x,z)
startGrid(n) -> [{pos,quat,slot}]   // behind the line, on the launch platform; slot 0 = pole
sectors []  (s values, strictly increasing, sectors[0] = 0 = start/finish; valid for BOTH main path and branches)
gates [{s,radius,kind:'ring',pos,quat}]  boostRings [{s,pos,quat,radius,power,duration}]  itemBoxes [{s,pos,lateral}] (cluster centres per orb)  hazards [{kind,pos,radius,...}]
branches [{id,name,fromS,toS,length,frameAt(u,out),halfWidth,risk}]   // shortcuts; project() reports out.branch
aiHints(s) -> {lateral, speedCap}   // racing-line offset and corner speed cap (m/s) for AI
attach(game) / detach(game) / update(dt, game) / dispose()   // attach adds group+lights, sets scene.fog/background, toneMappingExposure; update moves sky/water/clouds and the sun-shadow with game.player (or the demo leader)
```
`environment`: `{sunDir (to sun), sunColor, sunIntensity, skyTop, skyHorizon, fogColor, fogDensity, exposure, night, bloom{strength,threshold,radius}, grade{saturation,contrast,vignette,tint?}}` (the post pipeline reads `bloom`/`grade`).
Also export from `src/world/courses/index.js`: `COURSES` (metadata), `loadCourse(id, game)`, and **`previewCourse(id)` -> `{pts:[x,z,...], branches:[{pts}], bounds}`** computed cheaply from control points (no meshes) for the menus' track maps.
**Corridor**: the playable airway around the main path is an ellipse in the (right, up) plane with semi-axes `halfWidth` × `halfHeight` (typical 70–150 × 50–110 m; tighter in canyons/tunnels). Outside it the race applies a soft push-back; scenery should be shaped so you rarely want to leave it. Shortcut branches have their own narrower corridor.

### 9-F  Flight, input, camera, pilot (then UI)
Own `src/flight/*`. Deliver, in this order:
1. **`flight.js`** — an arcade-but-honest flight model. Keep the exported API. Required behaviour (tune numerically; document constants):
   * cruise ≈ 85–125 m/s (by stats), boost up to ≈1.5× cruise, minimum flying speed ≈ 0.55× cruise, **no hovering**; thrust vs drag give a stable cruise; **dives gain speed and climbs lose it** (energy exchange, scaled gravity ≈ 12–20 m/s²).
   * Bank-to-turn: roll input commands bank (rate ≈ 2.5–4 rad/s, bank limit soft ≈ 80° unless rolling past it); holding bank turns the nose (turn rate ∝ lift ∝ sin(bank)/speed); **pitch pulls along the aircraft's own up axis**, so a hard pull at 90° bank is a horizontal knife-edge turn and a hard pull wings-level is a **loop** (completable at cruise speed, speed bleeding over the top, regained on the way down). Slight coordinated yaw; `yaw` input = rudder trim.
   * Assist `standard`: when roll/pitch are released the aircraft eases wings-level and towards a gentle flight path (not during barrel/loops/tumble); `pro`: weaker levelling. Never fights a deliberate input.
   * **Barrel roll**: edge-triggered, ≈0.65 s 360° roll with ≈10–14 m lateral displacement in the roll direction, cooldown ≈1.0 s, sets `barrel.t>0` (evasion window). Double-tap bank key (≤220 ms) triggers it on keyboard (in `input.js`).
   * **Stall**: below ≈0.85× minSpeed the nose drops and authority fades; recovered by diving; push `{type:'stall'}`/`{type:'recover'}` events. **Airbrake**: strong decel (never below minSpeed) + tighter turns. **Boost**: `meter` drains while `controls.boost`, `startBoost` timed boost with power multiplier, smooth acceleration, `boost.active` for FX/camera.
   * **Stunt detection** → `fs.events`: `loop` (≥330° accumulated pitch rotation without ground contact, reward ≈ +25 meter), `roll` (≥330° manual roll, +12), `barrel` (+6), `inverted` (every 0.6 s while inverted ≥1.2 s, +4). Debounce.
   * `applyTumble` = uncontrollable spin with heavy speed loss and visible angular rates; recover smoothly.
   * Robust: no NaN for dt∈[0,0.1], quaternion renormalised, speed/angVel clamped, deterministic.
   * Node tests `tests/flight.test.mjs`: cruise speed per craft within range; dive/climb energy exchange sign; full loop completes and fires one `loop` event; barrel displacement and `barrel.t` window; stall recovery; stability sweep (random inputs for 60 s → finite); handling ordering matches stats (e.g. `sprite` turns faster than `bulldog`); `pilotSteer` tracks a synthetic 3-D looping spline within 25 m RMS.
2. **`input.js`** — keyboard (all bindings in §2), **gamepad** (standard mapping, deadzone, analog pitch/roll, haptics on `racer:hit`), **touch** (virtual stick + BOOST/FIRE/ITEM/ROLL buttons, built lazily on `pointer:coarse` or first touch; sits away from the top-left 112×44 corner; never blocks UI buttons), `pitchMode` setting, edge latching, double-tap barrel, blur/visibility reset, `input.enable(false)` for menus, emits `input:pause`, `input:camera`, `input:lookback`. Provide `input.bindingsText()` for the controls screen.

## 11. Addenda (lead, added at launch)

1. **Headless rule.** Node tests have no `document`/`window`/WebGL. A test helper sets `game.headless = true` (owner **R**: `tests/helpers/fake-game.mjs` exports `createFakeGame({ course, seed, settings })` → `{ THREE, scene, events, rng, settings, q, assets (has()=>false), params, headless:true, ... }`). When `game.headless`: racers get `new THREE.Group()` as `model`, and fx/audio/ui are not called. `buildAircraft` (C) must additionally not crash when `document` is undefined (plain materials fallback) so lab/Node tooling can still call it.
2. **Shared tree etiquette.** Five agents edit disjoint files in ONE tree and the game boots all modules together. Write complete valid files (never leave a file half-written across tool calls), `node --check` after every edit. If the full game fails to boot because of *another* role's file, do not edit it: retry in a minute, test through your own lab page/unit tests meanwhile, and mention it in your handoff.
3. **Browsers.** Launch Chrome only through `tests/tools/chrome.mjs` / `shot.mjs` (they kill only their own process tree). **Never** run `taskkill /IM chrome.exe`, `Stop-Process -Name chrome` or similar: it would kill other agents' browsers. Several headless Chromes share one GPU, so `fps` numbers you measure are pessimistic; report frame *milliseconds* and draw/triangle counts, and note when the machine was busy.
4. **No copying.** Do not copy code, models, music or textures from other Almanac games (`kart-racing`, `flight`, `vector-gp`, `night-cab`, `coil`, …). SKYBLITZ is a new standalone game. (Reading another doc to understand a technique is fine.)
5. **Handoff is progressive.** Create your handoff file as soon as your first deliverable works and keep it current; if you are cut off, the lead must still find the truth on disk.
6. **Textures are all on disk** (`assets/tex/`, with `manifest-a.json`/`manifest-b.json` describing size/tileability/colour space). `concrete.webp` is procedural; `smoke_atlas`, `explosion_atlas`, `aurora`, `facade_atlas` are the weakest — judge in-engine and replace with a better procedural version if needed (say so in your handoff).
7. **Windows shell.** Commands run in PowerShell/Git Bash on Windows 11; quote paths with spaces; prefer `node` scripts for anything non-trivial; bound long commands with `deltic timeout <secs> <cmd>`; run long jobs in the background and poll rather than blocking.

8. **Attaching to racers.** `Fx` and `AudioEngine` attach themselves to every racer when they hear `race:ready` (`{race}` → iterate `race.racers`) and detach on `race:end`/dispose; `attachRacer`/`detachRacer` must be idempotent. **R** calls `game.fx.detachRacer(r)` and `game.audio.detachRacer(r)` (non-headless) in `Race.dispose()` before removing models, and may call `attachRacer` for late joiners.
9. **Loading progress.** `loadCourse` (W) emits `game:loadProgress {progress 0..1, label}` between build phases (the UI shows it); `game.startRace` already emits `game:loading {cfg}` first.
10. **Course construction.** `new Course(def, game)` then `await course.build(game)` (see `world/courses/index.js`). W may restructure internals freely but must keep `COURSES`, `loadCourse`, `previewCourse` and the members in §5.3.
