# Review rubric — Chart, Compass & Calculus (higher-maths)

Scoring rubric for adversarial review of the Higher Mathematics revision site.
Each dimension is scored 1–10 by an independent reviewer. The release bar is
**at least 9 on every dimension**. A reviewer must list every defect found,
ranked by severity, with file, location and a concrete fix; a score of 9+ is
only allowed when no blocking defect remains in that dimension.

| # | Dimension | What a 9–10 looks like | Automatic cap |
|---|-----------|------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Mathematical correctness** | Every statement, formula, worked example, quiz answer, marking scheme and widget computation is correct; notation is standard (Scottish Higher conventions); rounding and exact values are right. | Any wrong answer or false statement: max 6. Any widget that computes a wrong value: max 7. |
| 2 | **Syllabus coverage and fidelity** | Every skill in the course specification (v3.0, May 2023) is taught; assessment facts (papers, marks, timings, formulae list, grade boundaries) are accurate; course-report quotes are faithful. Nothing out of scope is presented as examinable. | A missing spec skill: max 7. A wrong assessment fact or misquote: max 7. |
| 3 | **Intelligibility and pedagogy** | A typical S5/S6 student who passed National 5 can follow every chapter unaided: concepts before procedures, short plain-English sentences, worked examples that model exam layout, misconceptions addressed, good progression. | Unexplained jump or undefined term a student needs: max 8. |
| 4 | **Diagrams and visual clarity** | Static diagrams are accurate to scale where implied, labelled, legible at phone width and in dark mode; maths typesetting renders cleanly. | Mislabelled or misleading diagram: max 7. |
| 5 | **Interactivity and animation** | Widgets work without errors, are correct, teach something the text cannot, respond smoothly, and include genuinely clever animation; controls are clear; state stays consistent with the explanation. | Broken widget or console error: max 6. |
| 6 | **Exam alignment** | Worked examples and checks use exam-style wording and •1 •2 marking schemes consistent with official marking instructions; P1/P2 calculator expectations respected; command words, marking principles and timing advice accurate. | A marking scheme that would mislead a student about how marks are earned: max 7. |
| 7 | **Theme integration and identity** | The Minecraft × RAF Air Cadets × Napoleonic-navy mashup is present across decks, consistent with the Physics/Computing siblings, never obscures the maths, and there is a clear but subtle visual cue that this is the maths site. | Theme that confuses the maths: max 7. |
| 8 | **Accessibility, robustness and responsiveness** | No horizontal scroll at 390px or 768px; nothing under the "← Almanac" pill; keyboard operable; aria labels on canvases; reduced-motion respected; no console errors; light and dark themes legible. | Horizontal overflow or an untappable control: max 6. |

## Process

1. Reviewers work independently and adversarially: their job is to find faults.
2. Each returns a score per assigned dimension and a ranked defect list.
3. All defects are fixed (or rebutted with evidence), then the affected dimensions are re-reviewed.
4. Repeat until every dimension scores ≥ 9.
