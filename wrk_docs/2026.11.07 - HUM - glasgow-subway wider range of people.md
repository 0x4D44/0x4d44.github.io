# Glasgow Subway: a wider range of people, and no two the same

Status: approved by Arthur 2026-11-07, with these decisions:
- **Roster: 72 people.**
- **T-shirt prints: option A.** About 8 almanac nods among about 16 standalone jokes, the nods weighted to the **recent** sites.
- **Clothing range must include:** skinny jeans, baggy jeans, Lycra leggings, Lycra bike shorts, denim shorts, tube tops, high heels,
  trainers, flats, baseball caps, fancy hats, and **one top hat**, worn by one rarely picked person.
- My own calls stand: CC0 / CC-BY assets only, no novelty skins, ~8k triangles for the full-detail people, LOD file loaded first, and new
  people built in batches of about 14.

## What happens today

- 30 people (`models/people.glb`, 11.7 MB; `people_lod.glb`, 6.1 MB), built by `tools/assets_people_mpfb.py` from MPFB 2.0.18.
  9 hairstyles, ~35 garments, 3 teens, 6 children.
- A platform holds up to 36 people and each train saloon up to 24 (`js/sim/index.js:99`, `:272`). Typically about 60 are on screen
  at once, and up to about 84 when a second train is in. `AnimCrowd._pick` (`js/sim/people.js:273`) only makes a repeat less likely.
  With 30 bodies, a full platform must repeat people.
- The `adults in trousers` pool, the most-used one, has 14 bodies.

## Proposal

**1. Grow the roster to about 72 distinct bodies (from 30).** Each gets its own face (MPFB age, gender, weight, muscle and ethnicity
macros plus random face-shape targets), hair (from the 70 installed styles), skin (the ~30 realistic skins) and outfit.
Mix, by age band:

| band | now | proposed |
|---|---|---|
| children (6-12) | 6 | 8 |
| teens (13-19) | 3 | 14 |
| young adults (20-30) | ~6 | 20 |
| adults (30-60) | ~13 | 22 |
| elders (60+) | 2 | 6 |
| staff (hi-vis) | 1 | 2 |

The outfits are everyday Glasgow: tees, hoodies, polos, untucked shirts, jumpers, puffer and winter coats, cardigans, jeans, cargos,
dresses, skirts, school uniform, plus the existing kilts and Highland dress. Assets are **CC0 / CC-BY only**, as now. Each one's licence is
read from its `.mhclo` file and credited in `models/CREDITS.md`.

**2. No repeated person.** A registry shared by every `AnimCrowd` (the platform and each train) is consulted by `_pick`:
- **Guaranteed:** no body appears twice within the platform crowd or within one train.
- **Across everything on screen**, no body repeats while the roster has enough (72 bodies covers the usual 60). When it doesn't
  (a full platform plus two full trains), the repeat goes to the furthest crowd and gets a different outfit variant (see 3).
- A unit test fills every crowd to its maximum and checks this.

**3. Clothes variety at runtime, with Codex-generated textures.**
- **T-shirt prints:** about 24 graphic prints made with Codex's image tool. Some are jokes; some nod to the almanac's other sites (The Daily Flange,
  Opus Rally, Game of Dracula, The Night Cab, Stratego, Lost Valley, Iron Vertex ...). They are packed in one atlas
  (`models/tee_prints.jpg`, about 1.5 MB). At build time the script records each T-shirt garment's chest rectangle in UV space
  (`people_meta.json`). At runtime a small shader decal draws the person's print there, so any T-shirt wearer can wear any print.
- **Fabric textures:** a handful of Codex-generated fabrics (knit, denim, fleece, tweed, check) for the jumpers, coats and skirts.
- **Per-person colour:** each person's top, bottoms and hair get a tint drawn from a plausible palette, so the same body rarely looks
  the same twice.

**4. Load size.** About 72 people at the current detail would mean ~28 MB (main) + ~14 MB (LOD), against 18 MB today. Proposed:
- full-detail people at ~8k triangles (from 9.5k); textures shared across people where the asset is shared;
- `people_lod.glb` loads first, and the full-detail file streams in after the station is drawn.

Expected: ~22 MB + ~11 MB in total, with the first view no slower than today. This is an estimate; the first batch will measure it.

## What it touches, and how it's checked

Every new person must also pass the existing per-person pipeline: `people_skinfit.py` (the skin-under-garment repair, applied only where the
measured holes shrink), `people_lodhair.py` (LOD hair and brows), `tools/armfit.mjs --write` (arms clear of hips and skirts), and the per-person
budgets in `js/sim/sim.test.mjs`. Plus a contact sheet of every person as the game draws them, which I review by eye.
