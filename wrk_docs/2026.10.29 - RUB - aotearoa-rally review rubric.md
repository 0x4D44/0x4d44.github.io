# Aotearoa Rally — adversarial review rubric

Goal of the product: a browser rally game of "triple-A feel" — six New Zealand special stages,
championship against seven rivals, in the spirit of the late-90s rally classics — built on Three.js.

## How a review works

* Three independent reviewers (different lenses, same rubric). Each plays and attacks the build, then
  scores **every** category 0–10 with evidence. Reviewers are adversarial: they are paid to find what is
  wrong, not to confirm it. They are read-only (no edits, no git).
* Evidence is required for every score ≥ 8: a command run and its output, a screenshot path they viewed,
  a recorded number (fps, stage time, draw calls). A score with no evidence is capped at 6.
* Anchors: **10** = would not look out of place in a commercial release; **9** = polished, only nitpicks;
  **8** = clearly good, small visible gaps; **7** = solid indie quality, noticeable gaps; **6** = works
  but plain; **4** = rough prototype; **2** = broken in places; **0** = absent.
* Reviewers also log defects: **P0** crash/unplayable/stage cannot be finished/console error on load,
  **P1** major feature broken or ugly enough to embarrass, **P2** visible flaw, **P3** nitpick.
  Each defect needs a repro. Open P0 caps the overall at 6; open P1 caps it at 8.

## Categories and weights

| # | Category | Weight | What a 9–10 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Driving feel & physics** | 20 | Believable, readable, fun on all four cars: weight transfer, gravel slides you can hold, handbrake turns, jumps and landings, surface changes felt, damage affecting handling. No jitter, tunnelling, sticky walls or random launches. Keyboard, gamepad and touch all drivable. |
| 2 | **Stage design & content** | 14 | Six clearly different, recognisably New Zealand stages (distinct biome, surface mix, weather, time of day, length 3–5.5 km), real rhythm (flow, hairpins, crests, jumps, fords, bridges), good pace notes timed correctly, sensible boundaries, a bot can finish every stage. |
| 3 | **Visual fidelity** | 20 | Terrain, road, vegetation, rocks, sky, lighting, fog, weather and the cars look rich and coherent; textures are crisp and tile invisibly; wet roads reflect; plumes of dust are lit; no popping, z-fighting, floating props or seams; each stage reads instantly from a screenshot. |
| 4 | **Audio & co-driver** | 8 | Engine, turbo, gearbox, tyres per surface, impacts and ambience sound convincing and respond to the car; co-driver calls land at the right moment and never overlap; mix is balanced; nothing clips. |
| 5 | **Game flow, UI & HUD** | 12 | Title → championship → car/liveries → briefing → countdown → stage → results/standings → service → next stage, plus single stage, options, pause, replay. Clear, handsome, readable HUD (speed, gear, rpm, split deltas, progress, notes, damage). Works at phone and desktop sizes; no overlap with the Almanac back pill. |
| 6 | **Rally authenticity (clone fidelity)** | 8 | Reads as a rally sim-cade: time-trial against the clock and the field, stage points, co-driver pace notes, service/repair budget, damage, car classes, difficulty levels, ghost, replay. |
| 7 | **Performance & robustness** | 10 | Hits the budgets in `CONTRACTS.md` §0, loads quickly (< 8 s on a warm cache), no frame spikes on stage load, quality tiers + governor work, survives resize/tab-blur/context loss, zero console errors, no leaks across stage restarts. |
| 8 | **Engineering & verification** | 6 | Deterministic core, meaningful unit/property tests that fail when the code is broken, static validation, headless browser test that plays a stage with the bot, Almanac integration (catalog entry, shelf, back button, responsive test), original assets with licences noted. |
| 9 | **Polish & cohesion** | 2 | Consistent art direction and naming, loading screen, transitions, key art, favicon, copy quality, no placeholder text. |

Weighted overall = Σ(weight × score) / 100.

## Gate

Ship only when **all** hold:

1. The mean of the three reviewers' weighted overall scores is **> 9.0**.
2. No reviewer's overall is below **8.5**, and no category mean is below **8.0**.
3. No open P0 or P1 defect from any reviewer.
4. The lead has independently reproduced the evidence for categories 1, 3 and 7 (ran the bot over
   all six stages, viewed screenshots of all six, read the perf numbers).

If the gate fails: the lead sorts the defects by weight × shortfall, assigns fixes to the owning lane,
and runs a new review round. Scores are reported as observed; a round is never "adjusted" to pass.

## Reviewer lenses

* **R-PLAY** — attacks 1, 2, 6: drives all cars on all stages (bot and scripted inputs through
  `window.__AR__`), hunts for exploits (shortcuts, off-road corridor, stuck/flip cases, jumps launching
  the car, wall snagging), checks par times, pace-note timing, damage and championship rules.
* **R-LOOK** — attacks 3, 4, 7: captures screenshots on every stage at several `s` positions and
  weathers (via `__AR__.teleport`), judges texture quality, LOD popping, lighting, post-processing,
  car models, FX and audio synthesis (offline-render analysis), measures perf numbers.
* **R-SHIP** — attacks 5, 8, 9 and integration: walks the whole UI at desktop and phone sizes, tests
  keyboard/gamepad/touch paths, save/load, options, runs `npm --prefix aotearoa-rally test`, the
  responsive test for this page, the static audit, checks the Almanac entry/shelf/back button, licences
  and that no existing game was reused.
