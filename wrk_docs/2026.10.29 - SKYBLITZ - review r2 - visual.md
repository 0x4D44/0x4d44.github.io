**V9a is not a weak test, it is dead code.**
- A step up happens only when the median cost is under `upMs` (8 ms).
- At every scale the governor can reach, the floor is `0.6 / renderScale`, which is at least 0.6 (`governor.js:68`). So one 0.1 step predicts at most 8 x (0.7 / 0.6)^2 = 10.9 ms.
- That is under `downMs x headroom` (11.25 ms), so the check at `governor.js:107` can never fire with the shipped constants, and no test can see it go.
- The dead band alone keeps a steady load from oscillating, which my own governor trace confirms (V-9).
- I did not raise it; core may want to delete or re-tune it.

## 8. What I tried to break and could not

- **Chase camera.** I took 2,400 samples: 4 courses x 2 seeds x 50 s, autopilot (`E\r2\camocc.mjs`; `E\r2\camocc.log`, `camocc2.log`).
  - 20 frames were occluded (0.8 %): 16 by a wayfinding ring and 4 by props.

- **Input.**
  - Keyboard-only and pad-only both work through menu, select, race, pause and results (`E\r2\checks-a\checks-a.json`).

  - Left at the left edge stays put (V-26).
- **Settings.** Low, Arcade and Pro, picked on the real Settings screen, survive a reload, and the screen shows them selected (`E\r2\checks-b\checks-b.json`, settings).
- **Grand Prix.** I ran 4 races to the podium, used Resume GP from the menu, and checked the podium at 10 viewports. The buttons stay reachable at every size (`E\r2\ui-gp\`).
- **Context loss (E-12).** I lost the WebGL context mid-race at two sizes. The race paused, the notice was readable, Reload appeared at 9 s, and the race resumed on restore. 0 page errors (section 6).

## 9. What I could not verify, and why

- **Ctrl+W (V-2's worst case).** A headless page cannot show whether the browser closes the tab. Browsers reserve Ctrl+W, so no page can block it. That is inference, not a test. It is one more reason the airbrake should not sit on Ctrl.
- **The mix by ear.** The rules keep me off the speakers, so V-19 is measured, not listened to. Whether engine, cannon and warning pings still cut through a louder score is open.
- **Real devices.** All touch runs are emulated touch in headless Chrome on this box: no phone, no tablet, no real GPU frame times.
- **Timings.** Two other reviewers shared the machine throughout, so every time here is an upper-bound sample.
- **ui-screens 1c and 3a** (Resume rank at 390 px; touch and pad tips). Not checked.
- **E-08 in flight.** I checked the banner with the documented event, not a real missed loop.
- **Out-of-lens categories.** Flight feel, AI behaviour and item balance are scored from what I saw, at low confidence. One observation for Lens A: the autopilot player won all 8 of my normal-difficulty races (`E\r2\laps.log`).
- **`tests/responsive.test.mjs`.** I did not run it (129 documents, about 2m40s of Chrome on a shared box). My own sweep covers the game's screens only.
- **R2-P-01 with a real human.** A person may steer within the first second. `E\r2\idle2.mjs` tries the common input patterns, but not a human.
