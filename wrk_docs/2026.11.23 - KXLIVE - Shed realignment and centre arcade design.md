## Answer

Move the valley **6.147 m west**, from u = 36.105 to u = **29.958**, the mean of the line OSM draws between
platforms 4 and 5. Do it by keeping both published 105 ft vaults and changing how the footprint is shared out:
- the west wall moves **2.046 m west**, and the west aisle goes to 0;
- the east aisle widens from 4.10 m to **10.25 m**, and the east flank wall stays where OSM puts it.

Then stand a 1.0 m arcaded brick spine wall on the 4/5 island, from the floor up to the springing (5.64 m).
Platform 0 then lies in the east aisle, east of the main vaults, which is where Wikipedia (already cited at
web/src/shed.ts:63) puts it. Moving the whole shed 6.1 m instead is rejected: its east wall would stand on
platform 0's line.

## Problem, with an example

Today the valley, where the two vaults meet, lies over platform 4's track (OSM way 751608050, at u = 36.03 at
the buffer stop and 34.06 at the north end). A spine wall there would stand in the trains' way. The test
tools/tests/track-walls.test.mjs:70 already says so. So the model has no spine wall at all
(shed.ts:592, "the wall is not drawn below the springing"), and the user sees the two arches sitting on nothing.

**Which to trust.** The 6.15 m gap has two parts. **4.10 m is an artefact of the model**: the "aisle" is
whatever is left of the OSM footprint once the two published spans are taken out, and it is split evenly
between the two sides. **The other ~2.05 m is a real disagreement** between the OSM platforms and the
published span: with the west wall at u = 0, the 4/5 split is 30.0 m east of it, not 32 m. OSM also spaces
the west wall, the 4/5 split and the 0/1 split almost exactly 30 m apart (0, 29.96, 59.96). Platforms and
tracks under a roof cannot be traced from aerial imagery. The building outline can, so it is more
trustworthy for the overall extent. The tracks still win for clearance, because trains run on them.
Not verified: which platforms the real spine stands between. No source in the repo names them. OSM's split
fits, but OSM splits every island (2/3, 6/7 and 0/1 too), so that alone proves nothing.

## Options

- **A1, move the whole shed 6.147 m west.** Rejected. The east wall line lands at 66.06, with piers out to
  about 67.2. That is inside platform 0's envelope (64.31-67.91) along most of the shed. The west wall goes
  6 m into the Range's mapped rooms.
- **A2 (recommended), the same vaults with the aisles shared out unevenly.** The origin moves -2.046 m along
  u. `aisleWest` = 0. The vaults run from 0 to 64.008 in the new frame. The spine is at 32.004 in the new
  frame (29.958 today). `aisleEast` = 10.248, and `width` stays the whole covered footprint (74.256, with the
  east wall unmoved in the world). This keeps the published 105 ft (the repo already lets published figures
  outrank OSM, station.ts:225). It also needs the fewest code changes: every `width` user still means "under
  the shed". Platform 0's line (min 64.31) is 2.3 m east of the east springing (61.96), which is open: today's
  code puts no wall on the springing line, only the aisle-roof slab at 5.64 m.
- **A3, shrink both spans to fit OSM (about 29.96 m clear, R 14.98).** The west wall does not move, and the
  east springing lands on the 0/1 split to within 0.04 m. But it departs from the published 105 ft by 2.0 m
  per vault. The crown or the springing would have to change. Every `SHED_SPAN_WIDTH` user would have to
  change too.

## Spine wall spec

- Sources: Historic England list entry 1078328, Interior: a "round-arched brick central colonnade". Camden
  2009/5099/L p3: a "central spine wall" with late-20th-century timber boarding above it. Both are cited at
  shed.ts:581-584. **No dimensions found.** The 2026-11-09 ruling deferred "the central brick arcade ... until
  a dimensioned source exists". Choosing option A overrides that ruling, so every figure below is ASSUMED.
- Line: u = spine (straight along the shed frame), from v = 0 (the back of the south front) to v = L (the
  north gable). Base at GROUND_Y; top at `layout.springing` (5.639), so the existing valley fascia
  (`shed-valley-girder`, which starts at sp) sits on it.
- Thickness **1.0 m** (ASSUMED; today's valley fascia is 0.5 m). One round-headed opening per rib bay
  (`RIB_SPACING` 6.1 m, so about 40 openings), with the piers under the ribs. Each opening is 3.0 m clear,
  with jambs from platform top (0.9) to 3.4 and a semicircular head with its crown at 4.9, leaving 0.74 m of
  brick to the top. A 0.3 m stone impost and ring as on the south front is optional. All ASSUMED.
- Clearance at 1.0 m: east face 30.458, which is **2.12 m** clear of platform 4's envelope at its worst (the
  north end). West face 29.458, which is **4.05 m** clear of platform 5's envelope. Platform width left beside
  the wall: at least about 2.6 m on 4 and 3.1 m on 5 (the north end is the tightest).
- Budget: append it to the existing `shed-brick` batch (and impost stones to `shed-stone`). That adds
  **0 draw calls** and an estimated 3-6k triangles (not measured), against the 2M / 1000 limits.
