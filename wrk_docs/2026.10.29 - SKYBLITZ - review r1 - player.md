## 1. Verdict

1. It runs like a polished indie game: 57-63 fps, 0 console errors, deterministic races with 0 progress glitches in 30 seeds, and a smooth, readable bank-to-turn model when you fly gently.
2. The player's core verbs fail. Bank+pull corkscrews you inverted. The loop never pays its stunt. A human-like keyboard player finishes last on all 4 courses at 2.4-3.4x the winner's lap. Missiles land 19% of the time (0 of 8 for me), and every hit costs 6-7 s.
3. Races are close and busy, but the craft pick decides them (36% solo spread), and a respawn inside a shortcut gifts ~1.2 km of free credit. **Weighted total 6.75: a good jam game, not yet a polished indie racer.**

### P-01 | major | 1 (and 4) | keyboard path: `flight/input.js` ramps, `flight/flight.js` assist, `race/race.js:169`
- **Evidence:** I ran `keybot.mjs` for 1 lap, seed 7, normal, falcon, starting 7th of 8, with real key events (`RP\kb\summary.txt`). The human-like tap bot (0.12 s reaction, a decision every 0.1 s, taps whose length is the strength) finished **8th on every course**:

  | Course | Tap bot | AI winner | Grazes | Tumbles | Wrong-way |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | sunrise | 191.9 s | 56.5 s | 113 | 18 | 10 |
  | redrock | 172.4 s | 67.0 s | 73 | 6 | 8 |
  | neon | 132.4 s | 55.9 s | 101 | 5 | 6 |
  | frost | 158.4 s | 60.0 s | 77 | 5 | 6 |

  The ideal keyboard bot (key state chosen every 1/60 s, zero delay) did better but was still near the back: redrock 81.0 s vs 64.4 (**8th**), neon 65.0 vs 56.6 (4th), and a full 3-lap neon race 203.0 s vs 178.1 (**7th**). The same AI brain on the analog path won redrock in 64.3 s. `RP\kb\tap\shot-02.jpg` (frost, 1:07.9) shows the tap bot in a knife-edge dive at the ice while the AI has already finished. The tap bot has no anticipation, so it is a pessimistic proxy; the ideal bot is an upper bound no person reaches. A real person sits between the two, and the ideal bot is already last on redrock.
- **Fix hint:** Make the keyboard path forgiving:
  - A held A/D holds a steady, coordinated bank with the nose on the horizon (see P-02).
  - Small taps make proportionally small corrections (input.js ramps).
  - An optional line assist on easy.
  - Make the player catch-up actually work (P-13).

  Acceptance: the ideal key bot places 4th or better on every course at normal, and the tap bot's lap is within 1.5x the winner's.
- **Owner:** flight/input (with race/AI/items for P-13).

### P-02 | major | 1 | `flight/flight.js:150-154` bank command, and `flight.js:79` `rollRel()` (root cause is my inference)
- **Evidence:** The flight lab (`RP\flight-lab.json`) held D+S for 1.5 s. The table compares turn rate under bank+pull with bank alone:

  | Craft | Bank+pull | Bank only |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | falcon | 21.1°/s | 65.8°/s |
  | wasp | 41.6°/s | 75.7°/s |
  | manta | 2.4°/s | 55.9°/s |
  | bulldog | 4.3°/s | 55.9°/s |
  | comet | 6.8°/s | 60.8°/s |
  | sprite | 34.7°/s | 80.7°/s |

  The craft passes knife-edge at about 0.5 s, is inverted (up.y = -1) at about 1.0 s, and the heading reverses into a corkscrew. Real keys in game (`RP\maneuver.mjs`, frost; falcon, sprite, manta) show the same trace (`RP\man\frost-*-bankpull.jpg`). In real time on redrock at 153 m/s (comet), D+S for 1.6 s dropped speed to 49 m/s and altitude from 98 m to 4 m before the ground hit (`RP\rt2\redrock-09-bank-pull.jpg`). Bank and pull together is the first thing anyone who has flown a game plane does to tighten a turn. Hypothesis: `rollRel()` wraps |phi| > 90° to an inverted-relative angle, so once the pull carries the bank past 90° the controller levels to inverted.
- **Fix hint:** A pull while banked should tighten the turn (more turn rate and G at the commanded bank) and never roll past the commanded bank. Treat |phi| > 90° under a held roll command as over-banked, not as inverted. Add a test: D+S for 1.5 s keeps |bank| <= 85° and up.y > 0, with a turn rate at least the bank-only rate, for all 6 craft.
- **Owner:** flight/input.

## 4. What I tried to break and could not

- **Determinism:** sunrise seed 1 and neon seed 2, each run twice, gave identical hashes and results.
- **Progress integrity, 30 distinct races** (4 courses x seeds 1/2/7/11 normal, plus easy/hard/expert and 6 craft): 0 glitch bursts, 0 NaN, 0 respawns, lap and finish drift of 2 m or less. The neon seed 1 roof throw did not reappear.
- **Exploits with no gain:**
  - Skipping the loop costs 6-9 s.
  - Flying backwards over the line gives no lap credit (dist -1870); wrong-way shows after 2.6 s.
