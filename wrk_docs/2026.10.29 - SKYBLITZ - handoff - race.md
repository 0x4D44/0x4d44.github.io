## Design rules worth knowing

- **Progress is accumulated, never read off absolute `s`.** `racer.dist` adds the per-step change in the projected arc length. A step above `MAX_STEP = 150` m is ignored; 0.5 s of persistent disagreement re-anchors with no credit; `onTeleport(racer)` re-anchors only. So a teleport, a glitch in `project()` or a shortcut cannot skip a lap. A lap needs `dist >= lap * L` and every sector in order. Tests that place racers by hand must set `dist` and the pointer indices themselves.
- **Ranking** is an `order` array with 5 m overtake hysteresis; finishers lock in at the front in finishing order.
- **Wrong-way** needs smoothed negative progress speed, heading against the tangent, and more than 1.2 s since the last tumble.
- **Rocket start**: boost pressed in the last 0.4 s is a rocket start (perfect within 0.18 s). Holding boost as the window opens is an early start: `launchLock` 1.3 s and `racer:stall`. AI starts follow a seeded plan.
- **All damage** goes through `applyHit`. A shield absorbs exactly one hit; every landed tumble grants invulnerability at least as long as the tumble, so there is no chain-stun.
- **Rubber band** (`speedMulFor`): AI speed, craft normalisation toward the human craft (`cruiseNorm 0.4`), a gap-based factor, smoothing, draft bonus, EMP and launch-lock. The human gets a small `playerHelp` when in place 5 or lower. Bounds are tested.
- **Missile counterplay**: barrel roll (`flight.barrel.t > 0` within 70 m), shield, outrun (burnout after 4.5 s), obstacle (`course.collide`). Lock cone 0.42 rad, 0.9 s lock, 40..750 m. The storm strikes the best-placed unshielded racer and returns false (item kept) when none qualifies; the leader never rolls it.
- **Cannon**: 0.11 s interval, heat 0.075 per shot, cool 0.4/s (0.28/s locked out, resumes at 0.4), aim assist toward the best target in a 0.14 rad cone, five landed bolts within 2.5 s tumble the target.
- **Randomness** only through `game.rng.fork(salt)` (race, items, AI each fork their own), so a seed replays exactly (tested).

## API additions (beyond the frozen contract)

- `race.hit(victim, by, kind, opts)` - shared hit rules, for tests and debug.
- `items.threatOf(racer)` -> `{ missile, dist, tti }` or null (AI and HUD).
- `items.give(racer, type, count)` - grant an item (debug, tests).
- Event payload extras: `race:lap { racer, lap, lapTime, best, finished }`; `shortcut:exit { completed }`; new `drone:expire { owner, drone }`; `missile:evaded { missile, target, by }` where `by` is `barrel | shield | outrun | obstacle`.
- Extra racer fields: `radius, lock, empT, blind, draft, rb, shortcut, sinceTumble, heat, overheated, cannonCombo`.
- `race.stats = { glitches, relocks, overtakes, respawns }`.
- `race.results()` returns `{ standings: [{ racer, place, time, bestLap, points }] }`.

## Requests and API problems for other roles

1. **World**: `course.project()` remaps `s` at branch junctions with jumps of about 35 m. Progress tolerates it (branch transitions allow up to 0.35 L), but a smoother remap would make the shortcut-enter check less delicate.
2. **World**: `groundHeight` is the sea floor; R uses `def.waterLevel` for the water surface. Keep `waterLevel` in every course def.
3. **World**: R assumes the `collide` wall `normal` points inward.
4. **Flight**: there is no ground-contact hook, so R nudges the quaternion on a scrape. A hook would be cleaner.
5. **Craft / Fx**: `missile:evaded` carries `missile.pos`; use that for the dodge effect. `shield:block` has `kind`.
6. **UI**: the HUD can poll `items.threatOf(player)` instead of listening to `missile:warn` if it wants a continuous warning.

## Update 3: contact time, shortcut AI, behaviour review

Measurement added: `race.stats.contactT` (racer-seconds scraping wall / ground / water, finished racers excluded), `race.stats.racerT` (racer-seconds racing) and `race.stats.contactBins` (100 m bin -> set of `id:lap`), accumulated in `collision.js`. Scratch tool: `...\scratchpad\race\contact.mjs <courses> <seeds> <difficulty>`.

Changes: AI inside shortcuts follows the branch frame with a short look-ahead (`clamp(0.4 v, 32, 80)`), brakes for the branch curvature and for tube size (`40 + 1.7 min(halfWidth, 1.3 halfHeight)` m/s), and slows when beyond 50% of the tube; on the main path it recovers toward the middle past 70% of the corridor ellipse and caps speed in slaloms; a shield is raised when someone's lock exceeds 60%.

Numbers (3 laps, 8 racers, seeds 1/2/7, normal): main-path contact is about 1% of racer-time (sunrise 16.6 s of 1412; redrock about 21 s of 1993; frost about 20 s of 2127). Total contact is 4-16% and is NOT within the 0.7% target.

Cause (observed, World side): with the new `CourseLayout.project` "keep" rule, a racer that is merely passing a shortcut mouth on the main line keeps `proj.branch >= 0` for the whole branch window, so `course.collide` judges it against the branch's narrow ellipse (frost `u` 0-200 samples have e up to 2.5, redrock 62-124 progress glitches, progress max step 162 m). Proof it is not the AI: with shortcuts disabled in the AI, redrock still shows 18.9% contact and frost 21.0%. This also trips three tests now: `race.test.mjs` "progress" (max step 162 > 150), and `sim.test.mjs` redrock and neon ("field stays together"; redrock also has 124 glitches). The sim's spread and glitch asserts were left strict; they need the `project()`/`collide()` fix (decide branch membership by proximity to the branch tube, not a sticky previous id). Per-bin and contact assertions were not added to sim.test.mjs because the target does not hold yet.

Behaviour audit (what the AI does): personalities drive skill, aggression (cannon and missile eagerness), itemSense (reaction, patience, orb weaving), stunts (rolls and barrels on clear straights), shortcuts (take rate = personality, risk, place, difficulty), consistency (seeded wide / late / lazy mistakes, more at easy). Items: shield on incoming missile or on a lock, mines behind a follower or on straights, turbo on straights only, missiles at the lock or leader, storm only when behind, EMP when rivals are near. Rubber band: speed multiplier bounded (easy 10% to expert 5%), craft normalised, human help only from place 5 down, no gap-based cheat on the leader beyond the bound. Starts: seeded rocket plan, no gate pile-ups seen in sims (zero respawns).
