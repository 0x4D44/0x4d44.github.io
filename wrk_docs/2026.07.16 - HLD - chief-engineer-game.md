## 1. Intent & scope

**One line:** A self-contained browser game for the almanac in which the player
is the Chief Engineer of a cruise ship — running the diesel-electric plant,
fuel & bunkering, emissions compliance, planned maintenance, and failures up to
full disasters — across six stepped levels from a 200-pax expedition ship to a
96 MW mega-liner, with in-game tutoring for new players.

**Goals (become acceptance criteria, §10):**
- Six playable levels, each a voyage on a bigger/more complex ship; complexity
  gated per level (systems that aren't live don't appear at all).
- Realistic, engaging control-room UI: single-line mimic diagram as the main
  control surface, ISA-18.1 alarm annunciator, timestamped event log, ISA-101
  calm-grays-color-means-abnormal aesthetic.
- Real engineering model: generator lineup vs load (hotel + propulsion),
  spinning reserve, SFOC fuel burn, bunkering decisions, ECA fuel switchover,
  running-hours maintenance, failure events with causal chains (warning →
  alarm → escalation → casualty) drawn from real incidents (Viking Sky,
  Carnival Splendor/Triumph, Costa Concordia, rogue waves).
- Guidance: per-level objective checklist, a mentor voice in the event log,
  an in-game Engineering Manual, alarm→manual links. Learn by doing, no modal
  essays.
- Deterministic, testable engine (seeded RNG) with bot-playthrough oracles
  proving every level is completable.
- Catalog integration: `data.js` entry, sprite icon, shelves, shared back
  button, root test chain.

**Non-goals (explicitly out):**
- No 3D, no canvas ship rendering — this is a panel/console game.
- No LNG/dual-fuel modelling, no CII/EEXI mechanics beyond a report-only CO₂
  stat on the finale, no dry-dock/class-survey gameplay, no crew personalities.
- No multiplayer, no server, no service worker/PWA (see decision log).
- No free-play sandbox in v1 (levels only; the finale is big enough).

## 2. Game design

### 2.1 Fiction

The player is the new Chief Engineer of the (fictional) **Boreal Line**,
promoted ship by ship. A mentor — **Staff Chief Engineer Maren Voss** — rides
along, speaking through the event log and the objectives panel. Levels are
voyages; completing one (arrive at final port, ship intact) unlocks the next.

### 2.2 The six levels

| # | Ship | Pax | Plant | Route / duration | New systems taught |
|---|------|-----|-------|------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | MV **Aurora** | 200 | 2× 3.2 MW DG + emergency DG | Norwegian fjords day cruise (~14 h) | Mimic, DG start/stop & breakers, load & spinning reserve, alarm ACK, telegraph orders |
| 2 | MV **Baltica** | 700 | 3× 4.5 MW | Baltic overnight (~36 h) | Fuel tanks & burn, bunkering decision, hotel load shedding, maintenance tickets |
| 3 | MS **Meridian** | 1,500 | 4× 8 MW | Med 3-port hop (~3 d) | Purifiers & cat fines, running-hours PMS, repair teams, ECA zone & MGO switchover |
| 4 | MS **Atlantica** | 2,600 | 4× 12 MW, 2 engine rooms + bus-tie | North Atlantic crossing (~5 d) | Weather & sea state, LO sump/sloshing (Viking-Sky mechanic), redundancy/N+1, schedule pressure |
| 5 | MS **Caribia** | 4,200 | 5× 14 MW + scrubber | Caribbean loop (~5 d) | Hotel zoo (chillers, freshwater, sewage), full bunkering op, scrubber, engine-room fire procedure |
| 6 | MS **Harmonia** | 6,800 | 4× 14.4 + 2× 19.2 MW (96 MW), 3 azipods | Transatlantic repositioning (~6 d) | Everything + storm & rogue wave, safe-return-to-port fire, PSC inspection |

Voyage clocks are compressed (see §2.7); real session targets ≈ 8–20 min/level.

### 2.3 Core loop

Prepare (bunker/maintenance choices in port) → steady-state optimization at
time compression (lineup vs load, fuel economy, maintenance windows) → crisis
at 1× (alarm cascade, triage, procedure) → recover → debrief ("Chief's Report":
timeline, causal chains, stars). The design soul: **deferred maintenance and
thin margins author the player's own crises** — every casualty traces to
visible prior state, named in the debrief.

### 2.4 Simulation model (engine)

Fixed-step simulation, **1 tick = 1 game-minute**, all state a serializable
plain object, all randomness through a seeded PRNG (mulberry32), all inputs via
`applyAction(state, action)`. `tick(state)` advances one minute.

- **Electrical bus.** `demand = hotel(t) + propulsion(v) + aux`. Hotel varies
  by day/night sine + climate factor + events; propulsion `P = P_svc ×
  (v/v_svc)³`; aux covers thrusters during manoeuvring (heavy-consumer request)
  and boiler/misc. Online DGs share load proportionally to rating. Per-DG:
  state (stopped/starting/online/tripped/repair), load %, running hours,
  condition 0–100, LO sump %, fuel source. Overload (>100% of online capacity)
  starts a trip timer with preferential tripping stages (shed galley/laundry →
  HVAC) before bus collapse.
- **Spinning reserve.** Reserve = online capacity − demand vs largest online
  unit. UI shows N+1 status; losing the largest unit with no reserve = partial
  blackout. PMS auto-start assist is an unlockable toggle (L3+) — the player
  can run manual lineup for better economy.
- **Blackout & recovery.** Bus collapse → emergency DG picks up essential bus
  within a minute → guided black-start: start DG → close breaker → restore
  sections in order (cooling → fuel → propulsion → hotel). Drift timer near
  coast (L4/L6 scripted) gives Viking-Sky stakes.
- **Fuel.** Tanks: HFO and MGO (tonnes). Burn = Σ(DG kW × SFOC(load)/1e6)
  t/min, SFOC curve ~174 g/kWh at 80% load rising toward low load. Purifier
  throughput must cover HFO burn; purifier faults → cat-fines exposure →
  accelerated DG wear (visible in fuel-quality panel before it bites).
  Bunkering in port: choose tonnes per grade at port-specific prices within
  budget; tank capacity & minimum-reserve rules; a topping-off minigame risk
  (overflow → spill fine) on L5.
- **ECA / emissions.** Route legs flagged ECA. Inside an ECA every online DG
  must burn MGO (or scrubber running, L5+). Switchover is an action with a
  progress duration (~45 game-min); starting late or skipping → detection risk
  per hour → fine + reputation hit, logged in debrief. Scrubber: lets HFO in
  ECA, consumes ~1.5% power, can fault (forces switchover).
- **Maintenance.** Components (DGs, purifier, boiler, chillers, evaporator,
  sewage, stabilizers, steering, azipod bearing…) carry running hours vs
  service interval → due → overdue; overdue multiplies failure hazard and
  degrades efficiency. Jobs need a free repair team + duration; some jobs
  port-only. Teams: 1 (L1–2) → 2 (L3–4) → 3 (L5–6).
- **Failures/events.** Two sources: **scripted** per level (teaching beats,
  disasters) and a **hazard-weighted random pool** (seeded; weights scale with
  condition/overdue/weather). Events are phase machines: warning sign → alarm →
  escalation → casualty, with declared player interventions at each phase
  (earlier = cheaper). Catalog (§2.5).
- **Weather.** Per-leg forecast (calm/moderate/rough/storm) visible ahead on
  the route strip. Effects: propulsion demand +10–30%, comfort drain,
  stabilizer relevance, LO-sump sloshing hazard when sump < 50% (Viking Sky),
  rogue-wave chance in storm (L6 scripted). Player options: request speed
  reduction from bridge (schedule cost), secure for heavy weather, stabilizers.
- **Comfort & schedule.** Comfort 0–100 drains on: shed hotel stages, chiller
  out (fast in tropics), water rationing, sewage down, heavy rolling without
  stabilizers, blackout; recovers slowly. Schedule: required arrival times per
  port; speed vs plan; late arrival costs score/reputation.
- **Budget.** Fuel purchases, parts, fines vs voyage allowance → efficiency
  star.

### 2.5 Event catalog (initial)

Teaching/scripted: DG cooling SW strainer (L1), injector exhaust-temp deviation
(L2), purifier sludge-discharge fail → cat fines (L3), ECA switchover pressure
(L3), storm + low LO sumps → multi-DG trip risk (L4, Viking Sky), fuel-hose
leak → engine-room fire with full procedure: quick-closing valves → vent
stop/dampers → evacuate → Hi-Fog/CO₂ → boundary cooling; delay burns cable runs
and takes out an ER section (L5, Splendor/Triumph), rogue wave in storm +
safe-return-to-port fire + PSC inspection (L6).

Random pool (unlock by level): sea-chest fouling, injector failure, T/C
vibration, boiler flame failure (purge-before-relight rule), chiller trip,
evaporator output low, vacuum sewage loss, steering pump phase fail (swap to
No.2), stabilizer fault, bilge well high → leak isolation, shaft/azipod bearing
temp, oil-mist detector (stop engine NOW or crankcase explosion), off-spec
bunker (sample result quarantines a tank).

Every event declares: id, alarm tile + log text, phases with timers, valid
interventions per phase, consequences, manual page link, and a
`respond` hint used by the playthrough bot (also surfaced as the mentor's
advice on lower levels).

### 2.6 Scoring & progression

Completion = arrive at final port, ship intact → next level unlocks. Three
stars: **Safety** (no blackout/casualty escalations; alarms acknowledged),
**Service** (comfort average ≥ threshold, on time), **Efficiency** (fuel +
parts within budget, no compliance fines, maintenance not overdue at arrival).
Ship loss / drift-aground / unrecovered blackout at voyage end = level failed —
debrief still shows the full causal chain (losing reads like an incident
report, then Retry). Campaign progress + best stars in localStorage.

### 2.7 Time & pacing

Speeds: **⏸ pause / 1× / 8× / 32×** (game-minutes per real second: 0, 1, 8,
32). Auto-drop to 1× on any new critical alarm (speed control flashes). All
orders issuable while paused (pause-and-plan). Crisis timers are generous
(5–15 game-min windows) — playability over literal timing; the emergency DG
"45 s" becomes 1 tick.

### 2.8 Tutorial & help

- **Objectives panel**: per-level phase checklist ("Start DG2 and close its
  breaker") with live checkmarks; finishing phases advances the voyage script.
- **Mentor**: Voss's messages in the event log teach one action each; on L1–2
  they gate pacing (voyage waits at "Standby" until the lineup is right).
- **Engineering Manual**: overlay, ~12 one-screen pages (Power & the bus,
  Generators, Spinning reserve, Blackout recovery, Fuel & bunkering, Purifiers
  & cat fines, ECA & switchover, Maintenance, Alarms, Hotel systems, Heavy
  weather, Fire & emergencies). Alarm entries and panel headers link to their
  page ("?").
- Tooltips on all controls; "What's this?" on annunciator tiles.

### 2.9 UI

ISA-101 high-performance HMI: calm dark grays; color reserved for meaning
(amber warning, red critical, blue action-pending, green running). Single dark
theme (a control room at sea; deliberate committed look). Seven-seg-style
readouts for hero numbers (bus load, frequency), bar meters with normal bands
elsewhere.

Layout (desktop): header (ship/clock/speed controls/route strip with ECA + weather
+ ports/schedule/budget/comfort) → annunciator strip → main area: mimic
(center) + right rail (objectives, event log) → footer tabs for FUEL / MAINT /
SYSTEMS / MANUAL panels (slide-over). Phone (≤ 720 px): same header condensed,
tab bar POWER / ALARMS / FUEL / MAINT / SYS / LOG, persistent one-line alarm
ticker; touch targets ≥ 44 px. Mimic designed at 390 px first: DG cards top,
bus bar, consumer feeders below — interaction ON the diagram (tap DG → start /
stop / breaker / detail; tap feeder → shed/restore).

Telegraph: bridge orders appear as a brass-styled order chit + required speed;
player acknowledges (real telegraphs demand a reply) and sets plant to match.
"Request reduction" available during crises (bridge grumbles, schedule cost).

### 2.10 Failure UX

Blackout is not game over — it's the set-piece. Ship loss only via unrecovered
catastrophes (fire unfought, flooding unmanaged, grounding on drift timer).
Debrief always names the chain: "Blackout 14:32 ← DG3 trip ← fuel starvation ←
purifier deferred Day 2."

## 4. Testability design

- Engine pure & deterministic: `(level, seed)` fully determines a run given an
  action script → bot playthroughs are exact regression oracles.
- Events declare their own correct interventions (`respond`) → the bot needs no
  per-event bespoke logic; adding an event auto-extends bot coverage.
- Content validated structurally (no dangling refs) so authoring errors fail in
  node, not at runtime.
- Property checks inside playthrough: no NaN anywhere in state, energy/fuel
  conservation, alarm raised ⇔ threshold crossed.

| 1 | Vanilla JS ES modules, no framework, no build step | Site convention (shipshape/tidecall); testable in node directly |
| 2 | No service worker / PWA | Shared-origin CacheStorage footgun (lessons_learnt); pure online game; less scope |
| 3 | 1 tick = 1 game-minute; speeds 1×/8×/32× | Minute granularity fits voyage scale; crisis pacing solved by generous timers + pause-and-plan |
| 4 | Seeded PRNG (mulberry32), actions through one dispatcher | Determinism for tests and autosave/resume |
| 5 | Reconciling renderer, not innerHTML-per-tick | tidecall flicker lesson; animations must not restart each tick |
| 6 | Single dark theme | A control room at sea; ISA-101 grays are the game's identity |
| 7 | Aux systems (chillers, evap, sewage, boiler…) are light status machines with events, not deep sims | Depth budget goes to electrical/fuel/maintenance where the loop lives |
| 8 | Two-ER split + bus-tie only from L4 | Complexity gating; SRtP fiction needs it for the L5/L6 fire |
| 9 | No LNG, no CII gameplay | Scope; CO₂ shown report-only on L6 |
| 10 | Ship names fictional (Boreal Line); finale "MS Harmonia" | Wink at Harmony of the Seas without real branding |
| 11 | Levels only, no sandbox | Curated scenarios are the game (Stormworks lesson) |

## 6. Risks & mitigations

- **Scope creep** — the aux zoo could eat weeks. Mitigation: aux = status
  machine + 1–2 events each; the gating table is the whip.
- **Pacing/tuning** — compression vs crisis windows needs playtesting.
  Mitigation: constants in one `TUNING` block; bot asserts winnability floor;
  manual playtest pass before ship.
- **Mobile mimic density** — mitigation: mimic designed at 390 px first; tabs.
- **Alarm fatigue vs drama** — baseline alarms rare; floods only in scripted
  cascades (L5+), with a "critical only" filter unlocked L5.
- **First-contact overwhelm** — L1 shows 2 DGs and ~8 tiles, nothing else
  exists on screen (progressive reveal).

## 7. Acceptance criteria

1. `npm test` (root) green, including: engine unit tests, content integrity,
   **bot playthrough completing all six levels deterministically**, static
   validation.
2. All six levels playable in-browser start→finish; L1 teaches a fresh player
   via objectives + mentor with no external instructions.
3. Annunciator implements ISA-18.1 (flash → ACK → steady → clear), master ACK;
   auto-drop to 1× on critical alarms; pause allows orders.
4. Power lineup, fuel/bunkering, ECA switchover, maintenance scheduling, and
   the event system all live and level-gated per §2.2 table.
5. Blackout recovery, engine-room fire procedure, and Viking-Sky storm chains
   playable as designed; debrief names causal chains.
6. Autosave/resume mid-voyage works (reload restores paused situation);
   campaign progress persists; localStorage under namespaced key.
7. Responsive: fully playable at 390×844 and desktop; no body horizontal
   scroll; touch targets ≥ 44 px; overlay hit-testing verified via
   elementFromPoint (brilliancy lesson).
8. Catalog: data.js entry + icon + shelves + back button; no external runtime
   URLs; root test/build chains updated.
