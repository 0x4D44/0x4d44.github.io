## 1. Verdict

1. Not shippable yet: weighted total **7.25** against the 9.0 bar, no category reaches 8.5, and 5 majors are open in my lens.
2. The art direction, the original adaptive music and the menu shell are genuinely good. Four distinct worlds, a consistent -18 LUFS soundtrack, and a UI that survives 5 viewports, keyboard, gamepad and touch.
3. What holds it at "good jam / early indie" is finish. Resuming from pause fires your item. Ctrl-airbrake triggers browser shortcuts. Stunt popups sit on the craft. There is no shadow over water. The Neon atrium z-fights. The menu and results loops click.

## 4. What I tried to break and could not

- **Horizontal scroll and the Almanac pill.** 101 audits (20 screens x 5 viewports: 390x844, 768x1024, 844x390, 1280x720, 1920x1080) never exceeded 1 px of `scrollX`. No control sat under the pill, including the touch overlay (CAM/II top-right; `<` starts at x=112) (`E\ui-audit.txt`).
- **Keyboard-only flow.** Title, menus, aircraft (left/right changes craft), course, options (Up lands on the selected laps value; Left/Enter/Down work), hint, race, pause, results focus walk, Retry (the hint is not shown again), Quit to main (focus on Quick Race). No dead ends; 0 errors, 0 warnings (`E\checks-a\checks-a.json`).
- **Gamepad (stubbed standard pad).** Start/A/B/D-pad drive every screen, B backs out, the stick maps with the flight convention, A uses an item in flight, and Start toggles pause cleanly (`E\checks-a\checks-a-run1.json`).
- **Touch (CDP touch emulation, coarse pointer).** Tap-through of every menu worked with targets of at least 44 px. Floating stick plus FIRE multi-touch worked (roll 1.0 with fire, 6 shots). ITEM worked. II hides the overlay and Resume restores it without firing anything. The hint switches to touch rows. Rotating 390 to 844 to 768 mid-race kept the layout sane.
- **Settings persistence.** Low, Arcade pitch, Pro assist, music 0.5 and reduce-flash all survived a reload, and the settings screen reflected them (`E\checks-b\checks-b.json` settings; `E\checks-b\settings-after-reload.jpg`).
- **Camera under the water** (the clamp uses the sea floor). A realistic pull-up from a 7 m skim kept the chase cam at least 15.5 m above the surface (50 frames). A worst-case snap with the nose 50° up at 6 m left the camera 6 m above the water. Not reproduced, so it is not a ticket beyond the V-4 hint.
- **"Floating rocks" in my Redrock and Frost loop-side shots.** My own vantage was 81 m (Redrock) and 316 m (Frost) inside the terrain, and the Sunrise low-terrain cam was 51 m under the sea (`E\checks-b\checks-b.json` vantage). Withdrawn.
- **Audio robustness.** Live unmuted 3-race run (Sunrise, Neon, Frost via the real UI flow) with 0 errors and 0 warnings. The AudioContext stayed `running` on one context, 4 positional AI engines ran while racing, voices returned to 0-1 after each race, and peaks stayed at or below 0.90. `?mute=1` creates no context (suite). All 26 OGGs decode at 48 kHz with equal stem lengths, no clipping and DC 0.
- **Console errors.** None across the UI walk, FX gallery, 4-course visual runs, keyboard/pad/touch runs and the audio runs.
- **Grand Prix.** 4 races to the podium with correct cumulative standings. Resume GP after quitting reopens on Redrock (race 2).

## 5. What I could not verify, and why

- **Ctrl+W closing the tab.** Headless Chrome has no browser accelerators. V-2 relies on documented browser behaviour for Ctrl+W and on measured `defaultPrevented=false` for the cancelable combos.
- **How it sounds.** Chrome runs with `--mute-audio`, so every audio judgment comes from numbers and waveforms (LUFS, RMS, seam steps, live bus taps), not ears. V-6 and V-19 are measured, not listened.
- **Real devices.** No real phone (safe areas, notch, thermal throttling, real touch latency), no real gamepad (rumble was stubbed out), no high-DPI display.
- **GPU time.** Frame costs are CPU-side `renderFrame` times in headless Chrome; GPU time and frame pacing on a target machine were not measured.
- **Out of lens.** I did not run 3-lap manual races, AI fairness sims or memory growth across races (Lenses A and C), so my scores for categories 1, 3, 4 and 8 are indicative only.
- **Not run by instruction.** `tests/responsive.test.mjs` (the lead runs it).
- **Which audio test flaked.** The output tail cut the failing test name, and 3 reruns were green (V-22).
- **Photosensitivity under repeated strikes.** I captured a single lightning hit. I did not measure flash frequency when several strikes or hits land within a second.
