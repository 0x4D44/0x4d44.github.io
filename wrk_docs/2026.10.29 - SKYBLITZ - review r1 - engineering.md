## 1. Verdict

1. **Not shippable yet: 6 major tickets.** A respawn inside a shortcut keeps 650–755 m of credit. Both rocket-start instructions cause a stall. Enter on RESUME fires the held item. Moving hazards run on the render clock. Auto quality never scales back up. GL buffers, textures and heap grow every race.
2. **The core is solid.** 189/189 tests pass. 39 headless races (1072 laps) had 0 projection glitches and 0 relocks, and dist never drifted from position. NaN, reverse flight, mouth-hugging and re-entry loops gain nothing. Frames cost 3–6 ms, four rendered 3-lap races logged no console errors, and the deploy is clean at 21.0 MB.
3. **The tests claim more than they prove.** 16 of 24 targeted, non-equivalent mutations survive (25 tried, 1 equivalent). The survivors include the respawn, relock, junction, item-timing and settings code where the major bugs live, and validate-static misses six kinds of breakage.

- **Projection and lap logic under normal racing.** 39 full headless races: 4 courses × seeds 1–6 at normal, redrock and frost × 2 seeds at easy, hard and expert, plus 10-lap races on neon, frost and sunrise, 1072 laps in all. Result: **0 glitches, 0 relocks, 0 respawns**, (dist − position) never drifted (max 0 m), every lap completed within 2.7 m of the line, and standings stayed ordered every step (`ev\race-stress*.jsonl`). The lead's `glitch-find frost 3` also gives 0. Credited single-step jumps reach 77.8 m only at tight turns (sunrise s 2504 → 2581, redrock 1626 → 1672) and at the redrock shortcut exit (debt repayment). Each is telescoping-consistent (`ev\node-batch3.log`, `jump-find`).
- **Shortcut farming.** I hugged the branch mouth at 0, 0.4, 0.8 and 1.1 half-widths, ran re-entry loops (6 × at depths 60, 150 and 300 m) and left the branch sideways at the worst branch-to-main gap (226–437 m), on all 4 courses. - **Determinism inside one engine:** Node 3/3 (sunrise) and 2/2 (neon) runs bit-identical; Chrome 2/2 bit-identical in sim-only mode.
- **Pause during countdown:** the countdown freezes (3.33 s held for 3 s) and resumes correctly. **Hidden tab in a race:** pauses and suspends audio; the pause screen stays up on return.
- **Resize storm** (30 viewport changes, dpr 1 and 2, manual mode): GL buffers, buffer bytes, textures, framebuffers and three.js counts identical before and after, and canvas, renderer and composer sizes consistent.
- **Context loss and restore:** recovers with correct rendering and no errors (but see E-12). **No WebGL:** a clear message (`ev\probe\nowebgl.png`). **Blocked texture:** warning plus fallback, race plays. **Settings:** round-trip after reload works; tampered values (`quality: 'potato'`, `difficulty: 'insane'`, `masterVolume: 'abc'`) and corrupt JSON in both keys boot cleanly with sane fallbacks.

## 5. What I could not verify, and why

- **Real-time fps on target hardware.** Headless Chrome on a box shared with two other reviewers gives unreliable rAF pacing, so frame costs come from manual stepping with `gl.finish()`. Mobile, iOS Safari and Firefox were not tested.
- **Gamepad at runtime.** No pad is attached; E-03's pad variant is from code reading (`nav.js:82` + `input.js:79`).
- **A human-driven scrape respawn.** E1b used kinematic wall-riding through the real collision, contact and respawn code, not keyboard input. `contactRun` decays at twice the rate it builds, so a real player must stay mostly in contact for about 3.5 s; reachable, but how often it happens is unmeasured.
- **What leaks in E-06.** Buffer counts and bytes, three.js texture counts and heap are measured; which textures and JS objects are retained is not.
- **GPU texture bytes and audio-node counts** over long sessions; sessions longer than 4 races.
- **Hidden tab in title, finish or results states** (audio keeps running there by code inspection; not probed).
- **GitHub Pages CDN behaviour.** Case sensitivity is simulated by a script; cache mixing after a push (E-11) is inference.
- **Flight feel, visuals and audio quality** beyond what these probes measure; those belong to the other lenses.
