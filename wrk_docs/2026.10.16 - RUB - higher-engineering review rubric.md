# Review rubric — Rivets, Relays & Rigging (higher-engineering)

Scoring rubric for adversarial review of the Higher Engineering Science revision site.
Each dimension is scored 1–10 by independent reviewers (the dimension score is the **lowest** score any
reviewer gave it). The release bar is **at least 9 on every dimension**. A reviewer must list every defect
found, ranked by severity, with file, location and a concrete fix; a score of 9+ is only allowed when no
blocking defect remains in that dimension.

Sources of truth: Higher Engineering Science course specification v2.0 (Sept 2018, C823 76), the Higher
Engineering Science Data Booklet (Qualifications Scotland, Jan 2026), and the 2024–2026 course reports.

| # | Dimension | What a 9–10 looks like | Automatic cap |
|---|-----------|------------------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Technical correctness** | Every statement, formula, worked example, quiz answer, circuit/diagram and widget computation is correct; Data Booklet symbols and forms are used exactly; units, conversions, significant figures and rounding (no intermediate rounding) are right. | Any wrong answer or false statement: max 6. Any widget that computes a wrong value: max 7. |
| 2 | **Syllabus coverage and fidelity** | Every skill in the course specification is taught; assessment facts (papers, marks, timings, mark ranges, assignment structure, data booklet, grade boundaries, course-report quotes) are accurate and sourced; nothing out of scope is presented as examinable. | A missing spec skill: max 7. A wrong assessment fact or invented/misquoted report claim: max 7. |
| 3 | **Intelligibility and pedagogy** | A typical S5/S6 student who passed National 5 Engineering Science can follow every chapter unaided: concepts before procedures, short plain-English sentences, defined terms, worked examples that model exam layout, misconceptions addressed, good progression. | Unexplained jump or undefined term a student needs: max 8. |
| 4 | **Diagrams and visual clarity** | Static diagrams (schematics, free-body, truss, pneumatic, gear, flowchart, graphs) are accurate, correctly symbolised, labelled, legible at phone width and in dark mode; formulae render cleanly. | Mislabelled or misleading diagram: max 7. |
| 5 | **Interactivity and animation** | Widgets work without errors, are correct, teach something the text cannot, respond smoothly, and include genuinely clever animation; controls are clear; state stays consistent with the explanation. | Broken widget or console error: max 6. |
| 6 | **Exam alignment** | Worked examples and checks use exam-style wording and sensible mark allocations; descriptive-answer technique, command words, Data Booklet use, rounding rules, units, assignment advice and timing are accurate and faithful to the course reports; no claim to reproduce official marking instructions we do not have. | A scheme/advice that would mislead a student about how marks are earned: max 7. |
| 7 | **Theme integration and identity** | The Minecraft × RAF Air Cadets × Napoleonic-navy mashup is present across decks, consistent with the Physics/Computing/Maths siblings, never obscures the engineering, and there is a clear but subtle visual cue (copper masthead with rivets, dot-grid paper) that this is the engineering site. | Theme that confuses the engineering: max 7. |
| 8 | **Accessibility, robustness and responsiveness** | No horizontal scroll at 390px or 768px; nothing under the "← Almanac" pill; keyboard operable; aria labels on canvases; reduced-motion respected; no console errors; light and dark themes legible. | Horizontal overflow or an untappable control: max 6. |

## Process

1. Reviewers work independently and adversarially: their job is to find faults.
2. Each returns a score per assigned dimension and a ranked defect list.
3. All defects are fixed (or rebutted with evidence), then the affected dimensions are re-reviewed.
4. Repeat until every dimension scores ≥ 9.
