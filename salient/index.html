How to play

Goal. Conquer the map. Eliminate every rival power by overrunning their last territory.

Reinforce. Start each turn with fresh armies — one per three lands you hold (minimum three), plus a bonus for every continent you hold in full. Tap your lands to deploy.

Attack. Tap one of your lands (two or more armies), then a bordering enemy. Dice decide it: you roll up to three, the defender up to two, highest dice paired off — ties go to the defender. Take a land and choose how many armies advance into it.

Fortify. Move armies between neighbouring lands as often as you like, then press End turn.

Continents. Hold every land of a continent for its bonus each turn — Mirra, the centre, is worth most.
