# Teen garment objects

Original geometry, stitched details, swatches and geometric sun motif by the
La Ladera project, dedicated to CC0-1.0. The 29 styles and two size classes are
ported from commit `18a37a7e29888cecc733e16bcd623b21c599916e`.

Editable source: `la-ladera/tools/blender/garments_teen.catalog.json`,
`garments_teen_build.py` and `garments_teen_textures.py`.
The existing original fabric tile is `teen/fabrics.png`; no textures are
embedded in the GLBs. The adult and child shared atlas remains unchanged.

No third-party models, brand marks, product photographs, figure meshes,
armatures, animations or copied fabric designs are included.
