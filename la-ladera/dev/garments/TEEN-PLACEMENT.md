# Teen garment placement

The teen object library is everyday clothing for roughly ages 12 to 19, in two
sizes of the same 29 styles. Its models use metres and the same poses and pivots as
the adult and child libraries. The size is part of the design: `small` (about 12 to
15) is 70% and `big` (about 16 to 19) is 90% of one adult-reference pattern. Do not
scale a teen model again when placing it.

`js/interiors/garments_data.js` is generated by `tools/garments_data.mjs` from the
adult, child and teen manifests. A teen design carries `sizeTag: 'teen'` and
`sizeClass: 'small' | 'big'`, and its id is the style plus the size
(`tshirt_cream_small`), so the catalogue key is `garment_<style>_<size>_<state>`
like every other garment key. `CATALOG[key].sizeTag`, `CATALOG[key].sizeClass` and the
registered asset entry keep both. `CATALOG[key].size` is the measured
`[width, height, depth]` in metres. Select clothing by size tag and size class, never by
inferring age from a colour, gender or the household as a whole.

## Intended room kinds

| Room or container | Clothing selection | Pose and placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teen bedroom (household role `teen`) of someone about 12 to 15 | The `small` size class: tops, bottoms, dresses, hoodie and jacket, underwear, swimwear, socks, cap, shoes and sandals | Flat clothes on beds or clear floor; folded clothes in drawers or on shelves; hung clothes on a rail or hook. Check clearance with the measured state dimensions. |
| Teen bedroom of someone about 16 to 19 | The `big` size class, same styles | As above. |
| School locker or cubby used by a teen | The school set in the occupant's size class: polo, jumper, trousers or skirt, socks, shoes, cap | Folded clothes on shelves, shoes and cap flat on a shelf. Swimwear and dresses do not belong in a locker. |
| Adult bedroom or adult staff locker | Adult size | Keep the existing adult assortments. A household that contains a teenager does not make every wardrobe teen-sized. |
| Child bedroom or nursery | The child set (see `CHILD-PLACEMENT.md`) | A `teen` size is not a child size, and a child size is not a teen size. |
| Shops | Not part of this set | There is no teen shop recipe. |

School clothes are plain white, grey and navy without a crest, and make no claim to
represent a particular school's uniform. Choose only states present in each design's
generated record: the cap has no folded state, shoes and sandals have only a resting
pair, and socks have no hung state.

## Recipes

`teen-assortments.json` (this folder) lists the stock a container holds, in the format of
`garments-child/assortments.json` with two additions: a top-level `sizeClasses` (the age
labels) and a `sizeClass` on every binding and item. The file holds each recipe once per
size (`teen_drawer_tops_small`, `teen_drawer_tops_big`). Seven recipes, 14 bindings, 68
items; every one of the 29 styles is stocked somewhere, in both sizes:

| Recipe | Kind | Room kind | Hosts (catalogue keys) | Stock |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `teen_drawer_tops` | drawer | `teen_bedroom` | `dresser_4`, `dresser_low` | 3 T-shirts (cream, teal, sun print), ribbed and boxy crop tops, folded |
| `teen_drawer_bottoms` | drawer | `teen_bedroom` | `dresser_4`, `dresser_low` | chino, denim and cycling shorts, stretch trousers, joggers, jeans, folded |
| `teen_drawer_underwear` | drawer | `teen_bedroom` | `dresser_4`, `dresser_low` | plain briefs and boxers, socks, folded |
| `teen_drawer_swimwear` | drawer | `teen_bedroom` | `dresser_4`, `dresser_low` | one-piece swimsuit and swim shorts, folded |
| `teen_wardrobe_daily` | wardrobe | `teen_bedroom` | `wardrobe_2`, `wardrobe_3`, `wardrobe_metal` | T-shirt, hoodie, light jacket, short, knee, midi and maxi dresses, jeans, hung |
| `teen_wardrobe_shelf` | wardrobe | `teen_bedroom` | `wardrobe_2`, `wardrobe_3` | cap, sandals and school shoes, flat on a shelf |
| `teen_school_locker` | wardrobe | `school_locker` | `locker_pair`, `cubby_shelf` | polo, jumper, trousers, skirt, socks folded; shoes and cap flat on a shelf |

A recipe is a menu: a placement pass picks from it, it does not have to hang every item.
The file is source data for a later placement pass. Nothing reads it at run time, and it
is deliberately outside `assets/models/garments/teen/`, which has about 20 KB of its
700,000-byte budget left. `tests/interiors-garments-teen.test.mjs` checks every item
against the data module: the asset exists in that size, the variant is one of its
fabrics, the pose suits the container (a flat item on a shelf is a style with no folded
state: cap, shoes, sandals), a folded or flat parcel fits the footprint of every host and
a hung drop clears the host's height by 10 cm, and the hosts exist in the furniture
catalogue. A hung garment's spread width is not tested against the cabinet: the models
hang with their sleeves out, so a hoodie is about a metre wide.

## Runtime boundary at this base

All three libraries are registered and loaded by `loadGarmentAssets`, with the teen keys
pointing at `garments/garments_teen.glb` (one file for every pose; each key names its
`<key>_lod` root). A missing or broken file keeps the existing cloth stand-in. A teen
garment is one cloth: it takes the record's tint, like the adult and child clothes. The
detailed roots in the same file are not drawn in merged room interiors, and the fabric
atlas (`garments/teen/fabrics.png`) is not bound in the game.

This change supplies the library, its size metadata and its loader wiring, and nothing
places a teen garment. The `dressRooms` pass still uses adult assortments and skips
rooms of role `kids`, `child` or `teen` and rooms with a child's bed; it also keeps
schools neat. Drawer and locker contents at this base are the procedural props of
`contents.js` and `props.js`, which do not yet select garment models. The room-kind
table above is the integration contract for a later placement pass, not a claim that the
game has replaced those props.
