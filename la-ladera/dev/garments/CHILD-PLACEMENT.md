# Child garment placement

The child object library is intended for everyday clothing for roughly ages
6–10. Its models use metres and the same poses and pivots as the adult library;
the nominal length is about 60% of an adult counterpart. Do not multiply a child
model by 0.6 again when placing it.

`js/interiors/garments_data.js` is generated by `tools/garments_data.mjs` from the
adult and child manifests. Every design carries `sizeTag: 'adult'` or
`sizeTag: 'child'`; `CATALOG[key].sizeTag` and the registered asset entry retain
that tag. `CATALOG[key].size` remains its measured `[width, height, depth]` in
metres. Use the size tag to select clothing, rather than inferring age from a
colour, gender, or the household as a whole.

## Intended room kinds

| Room or container | Clothing selection | Pose and placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Child bedroom, explicitly occupied by a child around 6–10 | The child everyday set, including school clothes, pyjamas and swimwear | Flat clothes on beds or clear floor; folded clothes on shelves or in drawers; hung clothes on a rail or hook. Use the measured state dimensions to check clearance. |
| School locker used by a child | Child school uniform shirt/polo, pinafore, school trousers/shorts, cardigan, socks, shoes, cap and backpack | Folded clothes on shelves and hung clothes where a rail exists; shoes and backpack in their intact state. Swimwear belongs in a locker only when the activity calls for it. |
| Adult bedroom or adult staff locker | Adult size | Keep the existing adult assortments. A household containing children does not make every wardrobe child-sized. |
| Nursery or teenage bedroom | An age-appropriate separate set | This 6–10 set is not a nursery or teenager size. A crib or a `teen` role does not justify selecting these models. |

All models are plain original clothing without real school badges or brands.
Choose only states present in each design's generated record: rigid items need
not have a folded state.

## Runtime boundary at this base

Both sizes are registered and loaded by `loadGarmentAssets`, with the child
keys pointing at the child LOD packs. Missing files retain the existing cloth
stand-in fallback. The detailed models are available for previews but are not
loaded into merged room interiors.

This change supplies the library, its explicit size metadata and its loader
wiring. The existing `dressRooms` pass still uses adult assortments and skips
child bedrooms, nurseries and teenage rooms; it also keeps schools neat. Its
existing placement tests remain in force. Drawer and locker contents at this
base are a separate procedural-prop system in `contents.js` and `props.js`;
they do not yet select these garment models. The room-kind table above is the
integration contract for a future container or child-room furnishing pass,
not a claim that the game has already replaced those procedural props.
