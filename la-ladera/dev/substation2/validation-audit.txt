Scope and limitations to preserve in the PR:
  - This is an offline asset/data release. No game integration, first-person
    walk-through, hardware frame-time/draw-call measurement, live audio,
    door animation, collision navigation or runtime LOD transition was tested.
  - The yard checks selected phase spacing, bund size, perimeter heights,
    placement bounds and catenary crossings. They do not certify electrical,
    fire, structural or environmental engineering; the plan explicitly says
    the clearances are plausible and not engineering-certified.
  - Bounds are measured from binary geometry and checked against manifest
    bounds and authored metre envelopes. These checks cannot establish that
    every named equipment feature is visually convincing or mechanically
    connected. Contact-sheet/overview inspection is a separate review.
  - PNG validation checks format, integrity, dimensions and allocation size.
    It does not perform OCR, assess lettering legibility or certify material
    appearance. Sign orientation checks detect vertical inversion, not every
    possible visual error.
  - Anchor validation checks the required data shape and 100 Hz hum value;
    it does not simulate hinges, prove lamp placement or validate navigation
    around every polygon. The automatic gate does not compare the duplicate
    manifest anchor records with the catalogue or enforce materials.json's
    optional texture checksum records; the read-only audit above verified
    their consistency for this release.
  - The local GLB checker is an independent release validator for this static
    core-glTF pipeline, not the Khronos conformance suite or a browser renderer.
