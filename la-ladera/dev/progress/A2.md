## Plan layer, for A3 / A4 (conventions)
- Building-local frame: origin = footprint centre at ground-floor level, +x width, +z FRONT, +y up. `Room.rect` is the CLEAR interior (local).
- `Door.nx,nz` points from `rooms[0]` into `rooms[1]`; exterior doors have `rooms:[null, roomId]` and the normal points INTO the building.
  `hinge:'left'|'right'` = lower-u / higher-u jamb along the wall axis; `swing:1` = leaf opens into `rooms[1]`. Extra: `wall:{dir,at,t}`, `clear` (swept rect of the
  leaf on its open side, keep furniture out), `ext`, `side`, `entrance`. Interior doors 0.9 x 2.05 (0.85 min), exterior 1.0 x 2.1, shutters 1.6-3.2 wide.
  Arches (`kind:'arch'`) are Door records without a leaf; `room.openings` lists them too.
- `Window.y` is the sill height ABOVE THE ROOM'S FLOOR (not absolute); `Door.y` is the absolute local floor line of its storey.
- Stairs: ONE dogleg design everywhere (two 0.95 m lanes, divider, 1.0 m landing + 1.0 m half landing, rise ~0.175, run 0.27). `bp.stairs[i]` has `from/to/hole/width/steps/rise/run`
  plus `flights`, `landing`, `halfLanding`, `path` (local [x,z,y] waypoints for NPC walkers). Floors k>=1 have a slab hole over the whole core except the landing
  (`bp.floors[k].voids`); the room at the stair on floor k is `stairwell` (k=0) / `landing` (k>=1), flagged `core:true, noFurnish:true`.
- Ceiling clear height `h = clamp(fh - 0.18, 2.4, 2.9)`; slab thickness `bp.shell.slab`. Walls: exterior 0.22, interior partitions 0.11 (the gap between room rects).
- Roof: `bp.roof` = `{walkable, poly(world), y(world), style, rooms}`; walkable roofs have a `terrace` room list on floor index `F` plus a stair-head `landing` with a metal door (caseta).
  Unfinished lots have an open top deck (guards, no ceiling, `floors[k].open = true`). Terrace parapet and deck pretil are 1.2 m (`PARAPET_H`, `PRETIL_H`), caseta 2.45 m.
- Entrances: `bp.entrances[i].outside/inside` are WORLD points on walkable ground (outside = 1.31 m in front of the door centre line, inside = 1.0 m behind it);
  entrance 0 is the shop door for shophouse/cornerstore, else the main door. Entry steps (`bp.shell.steps`, walkable tops in `colliders.floors` as `<id>.step<i>`) fill the
  gap between a sill and the ground outside (up to ~0.78 m; typical barrio data is 0.12-0.28 m: one tread).
- `bp.look` = per-building palette hex strings `{wall, trim, roof, door, metal, base, stainy, seed}`. `bp.facade` = the list of dressing records (kind, side, floor, box) for
  the tests and for anybody who wants to know what hangs where.

## Findings worth remembering
- The kernel inflates every wall segment by R + t on ALL sides, so a doorway between wall pieces that stop at the jambs only has w - 2(R + t) of free centre corridor
  (0.9 m door in 0.22 m wall: 0.08 m). Colliders therefore stop `t` short of each jamb. `tests/arch-plan.test.mjs` pins the measured corridor to w - 0.6.
- The kernel ignores walls whose top is within 0.4 m of the feet: stair dividers / guards extend 1 m above the upper floor so a player standing at the top cannot slip over.
  The same rule makes anything up to ~1.1 m high a jump obstacle: a fence meant to hold the player in must be >= 1.2 m.
- Floors are point-sampled surfaces (no holes): compose slabs from rect pieces (`rectMinusRects`).
- The kernel CLIPS a move at a wall (no `blocked` flag when the push-out succeeds), so "walking into a door" is read from the player: velocity (`vx,vz`) high, distance
  covered (`speed`) ~0. `player.s.blocked` is only set in corners.
- A character that starts a step already overlapping a wall zone (tolerance 0.02) has every horizontal step rejected (`physics.js _step`): it is frozen, there is no
  depenetration. Anything that can leave the player inside a zone (a jump landing on low furniture, a teleport) can freeze it (REQUESTS A2 -> A1).
- Tour failures that were NOT doors: an NPC standing on the doorstep wins the aim (score d*(2.2-cos)) -> push-to-open; a prop box (A5, gas cylinders) 0.4 m in front of an
  extra door (see REQUESTS A2 -> A5, resolved).
- Louvre windows need glass behind the slats or every bathroom is open to the street (found by the closed-shell raycast test).
- Tintable materials need a near-white base `color` (A1 convention): a dark base times a dark tint reads black (doors, `tank_plastic`).
- `node --test <directory>` fails with ERR_UNSUPPORTED_DIR_IMPORT and runs nothing: pass the files or a glob.
