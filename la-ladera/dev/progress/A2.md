## Plan layer, for A3 / A4 (conventions)
- Building-local frame: origin = footprint centre at ground-floor level, +x width, +z FRONT, +y up. `Room.rect` is the CLEAR interior (local).
- `Door.nx,nz` points from `rooms[0]` into `rooms[1]`; exterior doors have `rooms:[null, roomId]` and the normal points INTO the building.
  `hinge:'left'|'right'` = lower-u / higher-u jamb along the wall axis; `swing:1` = leaf opens into `rooms[1]`. Extra: `wall:{dir,at,t}`, `clear` (swept rect of the
  leaf on its open side, keep furniture out), `ext`, `side`, `entrance`. Interior doors 0.9 x 2.05 (0.85 min), exterior 1.0 x 2.1, shutters 1.6-3.2 wide.
  Arches (`kind:'arch'`) are Door records without a leaf; `room.openings` lists them too.
- Stairs: ONE dogleg design everywhere (two 0.95 m lanes, divider, 1.0 m landing + 1.0 m half landing, rise ~0.175, run 0.27). `bp.stairs[i]` has `from/to/hole/width/steps/rise/run`
  plus `flights`, `landing`, `halfLanding`, `path` (local [x,z,y] waypoints for NPC walkers). Floors k>=1 have a slab hole over the whole core except the landing
  (`bp.floors[k].voids`); the room at the stair on floor k is `stairwell` (k=0) / `landing` (k>=1), flagged `core:true, noFurnish:true`.
- Ceiling clear height `h = clamp(fh - 0.18, 2.4, 2.9)`; slab thickness `bp.shell.slab`. Walls: exterior 0.22, interior partitions 0.11 (the gap between room rects).
- Roof: `bp.roof` = `{walkable, poly(world), y(world), style, rooms}`; walkable roofs have a `terrace` room list on floor index `F` plus a stair-head `landing` with a metal door (caseta).
  Unfinished lots have an open top deck (guards, no ceiling, `floors[k].open = true`).
- Entrances: `bp.entrances[i].outside/inside` are WORLD points on walkable ground; entrance 0 is the shop door for shophouse/cornerstore, else the main door.
- `bp.look` = per-building palette hex strings `{wall, trim, roof, door, metal, base, stainy, seed}`.

## Findings worth remembering
- The kernel inflates every wall segment by R + t on ALL sides, so a doorway between wall pieces that stop at the jambs only has w - 2(R + t) of free centre corridor
  (0.9 m door in a 0.22 m wall: 0.08 m). Colliders therefore stop `t` short of each jamb. `tests/arch-plan.test.mjs` pins the measured corridor to w - 0.6.
- The kernel ignores walls whose top is within 0.4 m of the feet: stair dividers / guards extend 1 m above the upper floor so a player standing at the top cannot slip over.
- Floors are point-sampled surfaces (no holes): compose slabs from rect pieces (`rectMinusRects`).
