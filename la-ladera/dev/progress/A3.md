## Design decisions (assumptions; nobody answers questions)
- Asset origin = floor-centre of footprint, +z = front (the side a person uses), +y up. Wall items: origin at the back face centre, y = hang height.
- Layout metadata lives in `js/interiors/catalog.js`; Blender builders take (w,h,d) from the same catalog (dumped to JSON) so manifest sizes match by construction; a test cross-checks manifest vs catalog.
- Tints are applied as VERTEX COLOURS (same material key, no extra draw calls).
- `room.finish` encoding (for A2): `{ floor, wall, wainscot?:{mat,h,tint}, ceiling, baseboard, trim, floorTint?, wallTint?, ceilingTint?, baseboardTint?, trimTint? }`; mat names are plain library names (NO '#'), tints are numbers (0xRRGGBB). Outdoor rooms (patio/terrace/balcony) have no ceiling.
- Anchors: sit/tv/work/read: x,z = hip position on the seat, y = floor (feet), `seatH` = seat height, ry = facing of the sitter. sleep: x,z = bed centre, y = floor, seatH = mattress top, ry = direction head->feet. cook/counter/customer: feet position, ry = direction the person faces. Standing anchors are always on free floor connected to a door; seats/beds are only emitted when a person can walk up to them.
- Furniture blocker walls are tagged `furniture:true` (and carry the item id) so NPC code can filter them; they are 4-wall outlines (t = 0.01), height 0.6..1.9.
- Furnishing RNG: `makeRng(hash('furnish:'+bp.id) ^ ctx.seed ^ lot.seed)` -> independent of how much other modules drew from `ctx.rng`.
- `bp.interior` holds my extras (household, layouts); contract fields `bp.furniture/anchors/lights` are filled too.
- Interactables for things lying on/inside furniture are registered at a point just OUTSIDE the furniture outline (front face), because `ctx.interact` rejects targets whose line of sight crosses a wall (my blocker outlines).
- Passage rule: engine keeps 0.78 m between blockers (inflate 0.4) and 0.38 from walls; tests walk with the real player radius 0.3 (`A3_BODY_R=0.35` for a stricter exploratory run).
