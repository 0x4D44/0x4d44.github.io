## Design decisions (assumptions; nobody answers questions)
- Asset origin = floor-centre of footprint, +z = front. Wall items: origin at the back face centre, y = bottom edge. Ceiling items: y = ceiling underside, the model hangs below.
- Layout metadata lives in `js/interiors/catalog.js`; Blender builders take (w,h,d) from the same catalogue (dump to JSON) so manifest sizes match by construction.
- Tints are VERTEX COLOURS (same material key, no extra draw calls). Reversed-Z engine: `polygonOffset` pushes AWAY, so coplanar overlays (decal on its slab) are offset 3 mm in geometry instead.
- `room.finish` encoding (for A2): `{ floor, wall, wainscot?:{mat,h,tint}, ceiling, baseboard, trim, floorTint?, wallTint?, ceilingTint?, baseboardTint?, trimTint? }`; plain library names, tints are numbers.
- Anchors: sit/tv/work/read: x,z = hip position, y = floor, `seatH` = seat height, ry = facing of the sitter. sleep: bed centre. Standing anchors always on free floor connected to a door.
- Furniture blocker walls are `furniture:true` 4-wall outlines (t = 0.01), height 0.6..1.9.
- Furnishing RNG: `makeRng(hash('furnish:'+bp.id) ^ ctx.seed ^ lot.seed)`.
- Interactables for things on or inside furniture must be registered just OUTSIDE the furniture outline (front face): `Interact.update` drops a target when `physics.segmentBlocked` crosses my blocker walls.
