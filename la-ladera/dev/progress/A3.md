## Design decisions (assumptions; nobody answers questions)
- Asset origin = floor-centre of footprint, +z = front (the side a person uses), +y up. Wall items: origin at the back face centre, y = hang height.
- Layout metadata lives in `js/interiors/catalog.js`; Blender builders take (w,h,d) from the same catalog (dumped to JSON) so manifest sizes match by construction; a test cross-checks manifest vs catalog.
- Tints are applied as VERTEX COLOURS (same material key, no extra draw calls).
- `room.finish` encoding (for A2): `{ floor, wall, wainscot?:{mat,h,tint}, ceiling, baseboard, trim, floorTint?, wallTint?, ceilingTint?, baseboardTint?, trimTint? }`; mat names are plain library names (NO '#'), tints are numbers (0xRRGGBB).
- Anchors: sit/tv/work/read: x,z = hip position on the seat, y = floor (feet), `seatH` = seat height, ry = facing of the sitter. sleep: x,z = bed centre, y = floor, seatH = mattress top, ry = direction head->feet. cook/counter/customer: feet position, ry = direction the person faces.
- `bp.interior` holds my extras (household, per-item lists); contract fields `bp.furniture/anchors/lights` are filled too.
