Fix, either one: (a) in `_variant`, right after the clone, `if (!base.image) v.version = 0;` (the loader callback already sets `v.needsUpdate = true`, which uploads the image); or (b) `await ctx.matlib.whenReady()` before the `start` stage in `js/main.js`. (a) is the smaller and also covers a late `materials` re-init. Re-check with the shot tool's `consoleWarnings` (must be empty).
