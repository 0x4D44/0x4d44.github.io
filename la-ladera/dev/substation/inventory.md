The two transformer families allow up to 6,000 near triangles. Every other piece allows 3,000. Every far root is at most 40% of its near root. The manifest pins all three GLBs by SHA-256 and exact byte count; the shared pack occurs once and both variants bind its identical path and hash.

Repeated fittings inside a compound tower use linked meshes within that country pack. This does not claim cross-file deduplication of every small fitting. The common standalone machinery, including transformer and outdoor switchgear geometry, is stored only in the shared pack.
