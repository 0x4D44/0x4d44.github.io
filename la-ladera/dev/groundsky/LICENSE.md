# Original Ground/Sky artwork

The newly authored Ground/Sky texture artwork, procedural drawing recipes,
catalogue descriptions and illustrative scene designs in this library are
original work created for La Ladera. They use deterministic mathematical
fields, hand-authored procedural shapes and seeded drawing operations.

No scraped imagery, stock photography, photographs of real places, branded
logos, copied skyline, real landmark or third-party texture was used. The city
is an invented Andean valley composition; it is not a reconstruction of
Medellín, Bogotá or any other identifiable place. Materials are visual
approximations, not measured surface scans. No image-generation service or
photographic source was used in this procedural edition.

the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**. See the

Python, NumPy, Pillow, Node, Blender, Arm astcenc, Khronos KTX-Software and the
system DejaVu font are build/review tools. Their software and fonts retain
their respective licenses; none of their program binaries is redistributed
as part of this library. The contact-sheet typography is review annotation,
not a texture to be loaded into the game.
