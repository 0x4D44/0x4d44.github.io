# Exterior kit: original work and licence

All new exterior-kit geometry, surface images, material recipes, catalogue data,
review assemblies and rendered asset illustrations in this namespace are original
procedural work created for this contribution. No scraped photographs, downloaded
models, manufacturer artwork, logos or copyrighted reference art were used.

These original assets and the new exterior-kit generation and validation code are
dedicated to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)**, to the extent
permitted by law. See <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>.

The water tanks are generic moulded domestic tanks with ribs, lids and plumbing;
they do not reproduce a manufacturer's branding or proprietary catalogue design.
The wall materials are generated from mathematical colour, height and weathering
fields. No generative-image service or third-party art asset is required to rebuild
them. The chalkboard surface is intentionally blank.

Existing repository geometry, export, priority and preview helpers are imported
and reused. Their existing authorship and licences remain applicable; those files
are not copied into this namespace or changed by this contribution. Blender,
Python, Pillow, Node and their dependencies retain their respective licences.

The offline review scenes illustrate potential placement. They are not a
construction, electrical or structural specification.
