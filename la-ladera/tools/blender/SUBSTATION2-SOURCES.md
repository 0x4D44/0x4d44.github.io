# Substation references and original-art provenance

Consulted 1 November 2026. These are shape, scale and component-function
references. No photographs, technical drawings, manufacturer meshes, logos,
product names or utility insignia were copied into the assets. All mesh
coordinates, textures, Spanish signs and equipment labels are original work.
The generated art is offered as CC0-1.0, consistently with the other asset
libraries; the reference documents retain their own copyrights.
The Reinhausen link was refreshed on 2 November 2026 after the earlier PDF
address returned 404; the replacement's system overview supports the same
component relationships.

## Art-direction choices

The 132/33/11 kV voltages and 40/15 MVA transformer ratings follow the brief.
The arrangement combines that equipment family with weathering appropriate to
an imaginary humid Andean setting. It is not a claim that these exact nominal
voltages or layouts describe any particular Colombian utility installation.
The two large transformer assets are one shared design instantiated twice;
the two smaller units are another shared design.

Grey-green paint, rusty edges, brown and grey porcelain, galvanised steel,
concrete, crushed stone, rubber, brick and oil staining are original
procedural surfaces. They communicate age and maintenance access without
inventing a real utility's livery. Spanish safety text is plain original
lettering, with no badge, seal, manufacturer mark or official insignia.

## Deliberate limits

This is a visual library and an illustrative layout. All clearances are
plausibility choices for game scenery. The data does not establish electrical
working distances, conductor swing or wind loading, oil containment capacity,
fire separation, lightning protection, earthing, step/touch voltages,
foundation design, fault ratings or a valid protection/switching scheme.
The 100 Hz hum anchors are the requested audio-placement metadata; they do
not generate sound and make no claim about the fictional network frequency.

The catenary utility is a geometric solver, documented in
[SUBSTATION2-CATENARY.md](SUBSTATION2-CATENARY.md). Its mathematical curve does not
replace a mechanical sag/tension calculation for a conductor, temperature or
wind/ice load case.
