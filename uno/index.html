👮 The Official Rulebook

  • The deck: 108 cards. Each colour has one 0, two of 1–9, two Skips, two Reverses and two Draw Twos — plus four Wilds and four Wild Draw Fours.
  • The deal: every player gets 7 cards; the next card starts the discard pile.
  • On your turn: play a card matching the top card's colour, number or symbol — or a Wild.
  • Can't play (or don't want to)? Draw one card. If it can be played, you may play it immediately; otherwise your turn ends.
  • Skip ⊘: the next player misses a turn.
  • Reverse ⇄: direction of play flips. (With only two players it acts like a Skip.)
  • Draw Two +2: the next player draws two cards and misses a turn. No stacking — that's a house rule!
  • Wild: play on anything and choose the new colour.
  • Wild Draw Four: choose the colour; the next player draws four and misses a turn. Only legal when you hold no card of the current colour — and the victim may challenge: if you're guilty, you draw the four; if you're innocent, the challenger draws six.
  • Say “UNO!” when you play your second-to-last card. Caught silent before the next turn begins? Draw two.
  • First flip: if the starting card is an action card its effect applies; a Wild Draw Four is shuffled back.
  • Empty draw pile: reshuffle the discard pile (keep the top card) and play on.
  • Scoring: the round's winner scores everyone else's cards — number cards face value, action cards 20, Wilds 50. First to 500 wins the match.
