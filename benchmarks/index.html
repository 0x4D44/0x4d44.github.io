BRIEFING // WHAT HAPPENED HERE

    
In June 2026 we locked fourteen model configurations in an arena: Claude Fable 5 at four reasoning-effort settings, the full Opus 4.5–4.8 lineage, Sonnet 4.6, Haiku 4.5, and GPT-5.5 at four effort settings. Every task was machine-generated from a seed, every answer verified by a constructive oracle — a brute-force solver, a hidden test suite, or the program's actual stdout. No human judgment anywhere in the grading loop, and an adversarial audit corps of 75 agents checking the graders' homework.

    
The headline result is not who won. It's that winning became impossible to measure with accuracy alone — and the moment that happened, an entirely different ranking emerged from underneath, written not in points but in tokens.

SPOILER // THE ONE-LINE VERDICT

    
Across 1,761 runs, no model ever beat Fable 5 at medium effort — and nothing matched its efficiency.

    
Seven contestants tied at a perfect 100.0 on the base tier. The championship tier broke the tie — not at the top, but from the bottom, as the oldest model in the field finally ran out of headroom. The survivors are separated only by how cheaply they think. That story starts in Chapter 01.
