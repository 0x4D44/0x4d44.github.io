No. They do not. Chained as paperclips, the arena's entire reasoning output stretches ~87 km — roughly London to Brighton — which is 0.023% of the way to the moon. The green sliver above is to scale.

You would need to run Fable Arena about 4,400 more times to clip your way to the moon. At observed prices, call it a few hundred thousand dollars of API spend — which, for "a chain of paperclips to the moon, made of pure reasoning," is honestly starting to sound affordable. That sentence is the whole chapter.

HONESTY PANEL // TOKENS ARE NOT DOLLARS
THE WRINKLE

Fable thinks in the fewest tokens but bills at frontier rates, so Haiku is still the cheapest perfect score ($0.032/task) and Sonnet the best mid-price ($0.057). Token-efficiency is a property of the mind; cost-efficiency is a property of the price list. The first is the durable signal — prices change monthly, reasoning budgets are architecture.

So who actually breaks first when the tasks get harder, and who falls apart when you think too hard? That's a darker story → Chapter 03.
