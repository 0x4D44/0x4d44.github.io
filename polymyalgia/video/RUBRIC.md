# Scoring rubric

Each criterion is scored 0–10 by an independent critic (a subagent that did not
write the code). It works from rendered frame contact sheets, the narration
text, audio measurements and the source page. **Pass = weighted mean ≥ 8.0 with
no criterion below 7.**

| # | Criterion | Weight | 10 looks like | ≤5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Fidelity to source** | 2 | Every number, claim and caveat matches the page; no new medical claims; schematic vs measured distinction preserved | Misquoted figures, advice the page doesn't give, caveats dropped |
| B | **Safety messaging** | 1.5 | GCA urgency, "never stop steroids abruptly", methotrexate weekly and "not medical advice" are clear and prominent | Missing or buried |
| C | **Narrative & script** | 1.5 | Clear arc, hooks, intuition before jargon, callbacks, plain English | List of facts, jargon-first, no through-line |
| D | **Visual craft / 3B1B style** | 2 | Dark canvas, consistent palette & type, meaning carried by shapes transforming, uncluttered | Slides of text, clashing styles, clutter |
| E | **Technical polish** | 1.5 | No overlapping/clipped/off-frame text, legible sizes, no glitches, consistent margins | Overlaps, cut-off labels, unreadable text |
| F | **Sync & pacing** | 1 | Animations land on the words; no dead air; 6–10 min; varied rhythm | Visuals lag speech, long static holds |
| G | **Audio** | 1 | Intelligible narration, original music that fits and ducks under the voice, SFX that punctuate without distracting, no clipping | Inaudible voice, music drowns speech, clipping |

Weighted mean = Σ(score × weight) / 10.5.
