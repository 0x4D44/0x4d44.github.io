NORTHERN LINEDRIVER

Seven cars. Two hundred tonnes. One wet railway under London. Take a 1959 Stock-inspired cab over the Charing Cross route, keep the guard with you, and put every stop on the diamond.

An atmospheric operating reconstruction, not a training product. Route geometry and timings are compressed; the cab and sounds are original procedural work inspired by late-1980s Northern line practice.

How to make a station stop

Brake early, arrive below 1 mph and place the cab on the white diamond. The guard controls the doors. When they close, wait for two bells, acknowledge with Enter, release the brake, then take power.
