# Historical notes and source trail

This is an atmospheric reconstruction rather than a training-grade rolling-stock model. The following points governed the scenario design:

- 1959 Tube Stock transferred from the Piccadilly line to the Northern line between 1975 and 1979, making it appropriate for a 1987 setting.
- The last 1959 Stock working in 2000 also marked the end of guard-crewed Tube trains; 1987 operation is therefore represented as a driver-and-guard workflow.
- The route uses the Morden branch, Charing Cross branch and Edgware branch station order.
- North End is an unfinished station between Hampstead and Golders Green, represented as a dark works platform easter egg.

Reference starting points:

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Underground_1959_Stock
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Underground_rolling_stock
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_End_tube_station
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampstead_tube_station

Cab details, timings, route geometry, signal spacing and physics are intentionally consolidated and compressed for playability. No claim is made that the browser cab reproduces a specific unit or working timetable exactly.
