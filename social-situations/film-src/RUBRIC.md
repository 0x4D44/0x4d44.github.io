# Video scoring rubric — "Social Situations as Learnable Systems" promo

Score each criterion 0–10. Overall = weighted mean (weights sum to 100). Target: overall >= 8.0
and no single criterion below 6.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 10 looks like | 5 looks like |
|---|-----------|--------|---------------|--------------|
| 1 | Gouache authenticity | 15 | Opaque, matte, chalky flat colour; visible brush strokes and dry-brush edges; paper grain; limited harmonious palette; reads as *painted*, not vector | Flat digital fills with a texture overlay; edges too clean |
| 2 | Stop-motion feel | 15 | Animated on twos/threes; "boil" between frames; cut-out pieces with real contact shadows; tactile, slightly imperfect motion; light flicker | Smooth tweened motion; no physicality |
| 3 | Story & fidelity to the site | 15 | Communicates the page's ideas (people-is-too-large, X/Y/Z axes, crowded origin, situation maps, observe→classify→respond→return, small repertoire, build/update a map, "not a diagnostic") with a clear arc and protagonist; ends with URL/CTA | Generic imagery loosely tied to the topic |
| 4 | Composition, design & typography | 10 | Strong readable layouts at 1080p; lettering legible for long enough and in keeping with the painted style | Cluttered or illegible text, awkward framing |
| 5 | Original music | 15 | Composed score with melody, harmony, rhythm and form that follow the scene arc (tension → clarity → warmth); pleasant timbres; clean mix, no clipping | Loop-y, static, harsh synth tones |
| 6 | Sound design | 10 | Paper/brush/card SFX synced to on-screen actions; balanced under the music; adds tactility | Missing, mistimed or too loud |
| 7 | Pacing & editing | 10 | Each beat long enough to read, none drags; motivated transitions; 45–90 s total | Rushed or sluggish, abrupt cuts |
| 8 | Technical polish | 10 | 1920x1080, H.264/AAC, A/V in sync, no artefacts/banding/pops, loudness ≈ -16 LUFS | Artefacts, clipping, sync drift |
