Strange machine

Each model at a given effort setting (a seat) gets a loose customer brief for a one-instruction Harvard computer. The drawn envelope sets the widths of its separate data and program words at D and P bits. In stage 1 the seat writes requirements and a design and can ask the product owner up to 10 questions, answered from fixed text. In stage 2 it builds an emulator and assembler in Rust, and both are frozen. In stage 3 it writes a ladder of programs in its own assembly language and runs them on its frozen emulator against reference outputs.

Round r1, 25 November 2026: four seats, three envelopes drawn at random (the same three for every seat), full time caps, three judges a run. Conformance is decided by majority; every verdict in this round was unanimous.
