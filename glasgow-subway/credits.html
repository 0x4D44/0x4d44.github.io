Credits & data sources

Under Glasgow is built from open data and open-licensed assets. Thank you to everyone listed here.

Map and terrain data

  • Buildings, roads, water, parks, rail lines, bridges and the Subway alignment: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL). The 3D city in this project is a derived database; it remains under the ODbL.
  • Terrain heights: Terrain Tiles on AWS (Terrarium encoding), a blend of public elevation sources including SRTM and national lidar datasets; see that page for the full source list.

Subway facts and appearance

  • Historical, technical and station information is compiled from Wikipedia and its cited sources, Wikimedia Commons file descriptions, SPT / Subway publications, Stadler documentation and other public references; estimates are marked as such in the project's research notes.
  • Reference photographs of the trains and stations were used to check proportions and details. No photographs are reproduced here.

3D models

  • The trains, tunnels, stations, signals, bridges and city landmarks were modelled in Blender for this project.
  • The people were generated with MakeHuman (MPFB2) using its CC0 base mesh and the community asset packs below; kilts, skirts and Highland garments are original.
  • 3D engine: three.js (MIT licence).

Asset-pack attributions

Everything used is CC0 or CC-BY; CC-BY items are credited to their authors.
