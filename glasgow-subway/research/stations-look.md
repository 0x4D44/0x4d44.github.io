# Station looks - reference for texturing (A1)

Source of truth for data: `data/station_looks.json`. Everything here is **described from reference photographs and text** (Wikimedia Commons / Geograph images, viewed for research and **not copied into the repo**), SPT's modernisation timeline https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/ , Rail Engineer https://www.railengineer.co.uk/subway-revival-an-update-on-glasgows-modernisation/ and HES listing LB32833. Hexes are visual estimates.

## The system look (2011-2020 refit, applied station by station)
- **Name bands**: continuous band ~0.45 m tall at 2.2-2.6 m above platform. **Orange (#F0601A) on the Outer-circle platform, charcoal (#3A3F46) on the Inner-circle platform**; station name in white bold sans (all of Bridge St, West St, Kinning Park, Kelvinbridge, SGX, Govan, St Enoch, Cessnock inner).
- **Line diagram panels** at the vault/wall: white header with SPT roundel and bold navy 'Inner'/'Outer', dark navy body (#1F2430) with next stops in white, orange square markers on an orange line, minutes at right (Cessnock, West Street, Bridge St photos). Hanging 'Inner | Outer' sign: charcoal block + orange block with arrows.
- **Roundel**: orange ring (#E8591A), white ring, dark inner disc with white 'S'; 'Subway' word beneath.
- **Floor**: large-format grey tile (#9A9B9D) with a cream-yellow edge line (~0.1 m) and a 0.6 m dark-grey (#555B60) tactile strip (stud pattern) near the edge plus a centre line of lozenge studs. Before refit the platforms had **tan/orange brick-bond small tiles** (#D9B779/#D9A860) with a dark-grey tactile band; some photos (Kinning Park, Cowcaddens, Kelvinhall, Buchanan) show that older look - those stations were refitted 2014-2019 so expect grey tile now.
- **Island-station vault**: over each track a curved **ribbed panel vault** (panels ~0.65 m wide, enamel, fine vertical joints) rises from the dado to a **flat soffit** over the island, with a **single linear LED/fluorescent tube** down the centre. Vault colour differs by station (white/cream/yellow/mint/silver/grey) - see table.
- **Box stations** (Buchanan, St Enoch, Ibrox, Govan, Partick, Hillhead): suspended ceiling of white/silver lay-in tiles with round downlights and long LED strips; white tiled walls; columns (black at St Enoch, dark grey at Partick); glass screens.
- **Tunnel mouths**: circular (3.35 m) bores into black, ringed by two or three courses of brick headers (dark brick) set in the end wall - best seen at St Enoch, Partick, Govan, Hillhead.
- **Track area**: grimy concrete/brick trough, third rail, yellow stencil 'DANGER 4 LIVE RAIL 4', standing water between the rails (West Street, Kelvinbridge, SGX).
- **Platform screen doors** (Gilgen, 1.7 m): white frames, clear glass panels, **orange door leaves**; first at Govan (2025), then Partick and Ibrox (to Sept 2026).
- **Entrances**: 1970s-80s dark-brown brick pavilions with a flat grey canopy fascia carrying the roundel and 'Subway' (Kinning Park, Cowcaddens, Shields Road), glass canopies at the busy city stations (St Enoch, Buchanan, Govan), shopfront (Kelvinhall) and tenement basement (Cessnock).

## Per-station notes
### Buchanan Street
- Features: underground ticket hall; travelator to Queen Street station; one stair per platform (congested); glass screen on island platform; two street entrances mid-street (north has escalators).
- Entrance: Teal-tinted glass box with white capital lettering "BUCHANAN STREET" on the fascia; steel/glass balustrade; black totem with SPT roundel and "Subway"; polished steel bollards with black bands.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Buchanan_Street_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buchanan_Street_subway_station, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

### St Enoch
- Features: two escalator flights from street (deepest city-centre station); lift (one of two accessible stations); black glossy tile columns; safety fence between tracks.
- Entrance: Red sandstone (Locharbriggs) Flemish Renaissance two-storey block, pepper-pot corner turrets with swept roofs, polished ashlar; now a cafe. New glass canopies at Argyle Street and north/south entrances.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:St_Enoch_subway_station, https://www.trove.scot/designation/LB32833, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Enoch_subway_station

### Bridge Street
- Features: island platform; orange band on Outer side, charcoal band on Inner side; wet track bed.
- Entrance: not photographed in this research; assume low building with grey fascia and SPT roundel.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Bridge_Street_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bridge_Street_subway_station, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

### West Street
- Features: yellow vault is unique to West Street; least used station; orange band on Outer side, charcoal on Inner.
- Entrance: not photographed; assume low brick/grey building.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:West_Street_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Street_subway_station

### Shields Road
- Features: Park and Ride (839 spaces) with multi-storey car park; refit completed March 2018.
- Entrance: Dark brown brick walls (#4B342C), grey metal canopy/fascia with orange roundel and "Subway", glazed doors; a painted mural on the building depicts Mackintosh's Scotland Street School.
- Mural/art: Painted mural of Scotland Street School (Mackintosh) on the station building (Commons geograph 1523759 description).
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Shields_Road_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shields_Road_subway_station

### Kinning Park
- Features: M8 beside the entrance; refit completed Nov 2019 (photo may predate it).
- Entrance: Plain dark brick (#3E2C26) with grey fascia and orange/white roundel + "Subway" lettering.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Kinning_Park_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinning_Park_subway_station

### Cessnock
- Features: no escalators - stairs only; retained pre-modernisation signage (partly); refit completed Dec 2016.
- Entrance: Stone steps flanked by two black cast iron arches with the station name (1989); sandstone tenement above.
- Mural/art: Ink street-scene drawing of Thomson's Walmer Crescent on a vault panel (geograph 5909755).
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Cessnock_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cessnock_subway_station

### Ibrox
- Features: glass barrier between the tracks; match-day second entrance on Woodville Street; reopened Jan 2014 as first revamped south-side station.
- Entrance: Details not confirmed (Faith47 mural nearby on Copland Road).
- Mural/art: Faith47 mural on Copland Road opposite Fairley Street (2014, street-side).
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Ibrox_subway_station, https://www.scotsman.com/news/transport/latest-revamped-glasgow-subway-station-unveiled-1547004

### Govan
- Features: lift + escalator (accessible); first station with platform screen doors (2025); depot Broomloan just to the south.
- Entrance: Dark grey cladded box with a domed/curved glass canopy over the entrance and a glazed Golspie Street entrance; stone paving; bronze sculpture group in the square; Govan Stones banners nearby.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Govan_subway_station, https://www.ahr.co.uk/projects/govan-subway-and-bus-interchange, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

### Partick
- Features: platform screen doors (white frames, orange door leaves) installed 2025-26; interchange concourse to National Rail; octagonal columns with an orange stripe band and pale-blue bases.
- Entrance: Large hall: pale cream floor tiles with grey inlay, white round pillars with blue bands, circular ceiling roses, stainless steel cladding, Costa kiosk.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Partick_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partick_station, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

### Kelvinhall
- Features: no escalators; public art by Paul Buchanan (2014); refit completed April 2014 (look may have changed).
- Entrance: Glazed shopfront with a white fascia box reading "Kelvinhall" in orange with the SPT logo; sandstone tenement above (#B0603E).
- Mural/art: Public art by Glasgow musician and artist Paul Buchanan (2014) - not described in sources.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Kelvinhall_subway_station, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

### Hillhead
- Features: first station modernised (opened Sept 2012); two escalators + stair; deepest tunnels beyond; dark dado tiles.
- Entrance: Ticket hall with the Alasdair Gray ceramic mural (about 2 m high x 12 m wide) of Hillhead streets and landmarks (University, Kelvingrove, Botanic Gardens) and figure panels, jigsaw tiles with grey grout.
- Mural/art: Alasdair Gray & Nichol Wheatley ceramic mural, Sadler Green tiles, unveiled 16 Sept 2012.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Hillhead_subway_station, http://www.sadlergreen.com/blog/glasgow-hillhead-station-mural/, https://www.culturematters.org.uk/a-well-built-wall/

### Kelvinbridge
- Features: deepest station; pumps run continuously; glass-enclosed escalator; old cast-iron stair retained as emergency exit; refit completed July 2017.
- Entrance: Glazed escalator enclosure; red doors at the foot of the old cast iron stair.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Kelvinbridge_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvinbridge_subway_station

### St George's Cross
- Features: only station rebuilt before 1977 (1971 surface building); heated using recycled tunnel water (SPT); refit completed June 2019.
- Entrance: Low building at the Cross with stairs down to Great Western Road side; modern SPT roundel signage.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:St_George%27s_Cross_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George's_Cross_subway_station, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

### Cowcaddens
- Features: Dundasvale traction sub-station; refit completed 2019.
- Entrance: Dark brick (#3E2C26) with a grey fascia carrying the roundel and "Subway"; paved forecourt.
- Mural/art: none documented.
- Sources: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Cowcaddens_subway_station, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cowcaddens_subway_station, https://www.spt.co.uk/about-us/what-we-are-doing/modernisation/

## Caveats
- Photos are of different dates: pre-refit finishes (yellow Kelvinhall, tan floors, mint Cowcaddens) probably no longer exist; the post-refit stations seen (Bridge St, West St, Kelvinbridge, SGX, Govan, Partick, St Enoch, Hillhead, Ibrox) use the white/grey kit above.
- Wikipedia's SGX text says brown brick and orange tile survive; photos of the platform show cream panels with orange/charcoal bands (2019 refit), so the photos win.
- No photos were found for Bridge Street entrance, West Street entrance, Ibrox entrance, Hillhead entrance - described as assumptions in the JSON (confidence medium/low).
