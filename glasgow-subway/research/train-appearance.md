# Current Glasgow Subway train (Stadler, 3rd generation) - appearance brief for modelling

Compiled by A1 for A3. Most of this was read **directly from photographs** on Wikimedia Commons (viewed during research) plus trade-press text; where only a photo says it, the file is named. Hex colours are visual estimates from photos, not SPT brand specs.

Units 301-317, 4 cars, in passenger service since 11 Dec 2023, only fleet since 28 Jun 2024 (the orange Metro-Cammell stock is gone). "Not so orange": the new livery is **predominantly white** with orange accents (The Scotsman; Londonist compared it to London Overground's white/orange).

## 1. Overall form and dimensions
- 4-car articulated set, **39.25 m** (sources 39.2-39.51), width **2.31-2.34 m** over body, height **2.64-2.65 m**, floor height **0.695 m** above rail head. Extruded aluminium body. Described as a "linear tube no higher than a standard doorway" (DCA Design); "hobbit metro" at InnoTrans.
- UIC Bo'Bo' + 2' + 2' + Bo'Bo': the set is **two identical half-trains coupled in the middle** (shell-sleeve coupler, Commons J33 699/707). Each half = a **power end car with two motor bogies** (one under the cab end, one at the articulation) plus a **trailer car whose single bogie sits at the mid-set end**; the power car's articulation bogie carries one end of the trailer. Total **6 bogies**; suggested centres from the front (ESTIMATE): 3.4, 11.0, 17.0, 22.3, 28.3, 35.9 m. Wheelbase only 1.65 m, wheels 480-540 mm, so bogies are small and mostly hidden behind the black skirt.
- Body is a smooth **extruded tube with a rounded-rectangle cross-section**: vertical sides in the lower half, curved shoulder, domed roof (see section 4). Very little roof equipment is visible in photos (cars are so low that the roof is practically a smooth cap; a shallow lighter-grey roof panel strip is visible along the top edge; no pantograph - third rail).
- Wheels 540-480 mm dia, wheelbase 1650 mm, air secondary suspension, resilient wheels. Solebar/skirt: a **black underframe band** about 0.35 m deep along the whole lower body, below which bogies are visible (bogies are open, black/grey, no skirts).

## 2. Livery (exterior)
- Body: **gloss white** (#F2F1EC, slightly warm; looks cream-grey in tunnel light and streaks with grime).
- **Door zones: bright orange** (#F26B1D to #E8590C; between "SPT orange" and a safety orange) - the whole door leaf panels and a ~0.5 m vertical orange band either side of each door opening, running from the roof curve down to the black skirt (clearly visible in InnoTrans photo: orange band flares slightly at the top shoulder). Later (2024-25) trains appear to have a **narrower orange door-frame outline plus orange door leaves**, white body between doors.
- **Black window band**: each side window is a very large rounded-rectangle opening with a **black gloss surround** (looks like bonded glazing in a black frame). Two windows per bay between doors; they read as a continuous dark ribbon. Glass is slightly tinted.
- **Cab front**: white fibre-glass nose with a **big black rounded-rectangle glazing panel** covering most of the front face (windscreen and the central evacuation door are all in one black panel). The glazing is blacked out/privacy tinted for the driver (busandtrainuser). SPT plans to remove the cab and open the view (DLR-style) in UTO.
- **SPT roundel**: orange ring with a white/grey inner ring and a white "S" ("S" in a roundel), word **"Subway"** in orange beneath it. One roundel on the **front, centre-lower of the black panel** (~0.35 m dia, near the bottom of the glazing) and one on each side of the end car near the first door (~0.3 m), ahead of a small orange "SPT" logo and the car number (e.g. 304, 305, 306, 307) at the lower side/front.
- Small orange rectangular **handle/inspection hatch** at the lower side ends (Commons 2024 918 and 921 show orange rectangle at skirt level near the car end).
- Dirt/white streaks are normal on in-service units (optional weathering).

## 3. Nose / cab front details
- Plan shape: **rounded/bulbous nose, slight forward overhang above the bumper**, front face nearly vertical with large radius corners. Roof line curves over with a large radius; the nose is not pointed. Width at the front ~ full body width.
- Black glazing panel ~ 1.9 m wide x 1.35 m tall, starts ~0.55 m above rail level... roughly from 0.7 m to 2.0 m above rail level, rounded corners R~0.25 m. Includes a **central front emergency evacuation door**, hinged at the right as seen from outside (Commons J33 728/695: the door swings open to reveal the saloon; it is a framed black glass door with grey inner panel).
- **Headlights/marker lights**: two tall slim **vertical LED light strips** (warm white, "eyebrow" blades) running up the inside left/right edges of the black panel, each ~0.9 m long, curving to follow the panel edge, with a **cluster of 8 small LED dots (2x4)** at the bottom of each strip. Reverse set: **red tail lights** (same strips show red - Commons `StadlerRear-RedLights`). No separate round headlamps.
- **Coupler / bumper**: dark grey Scharfenberg coupler housing with electrical contact blocks on either side, at ~0.55 m above rail; a flat black/grey bumper plate (width ~ 1.2 m) below the black glazing. Small orange grab handle on the lower bodywork above it.
- Cab front **doesn't have the roof destination blind**: no indicator is visible on the exterior; fleet number and roundel only.

## 4. Cross-section (to build the body and the saloon vault)
(All figures = photo estimates anchored to 2.31 m width / 2.64 m height.)
- Body outer: width 2.31 m. Lower body vertical sides from the skirt (~0.45 m above rail) up to ~1.30 m; curved shoulder from 1.30 m to 2.64 m (dome). Roof radius ~1.2 m near the sides, flattening at the centre.
- Floor height 0.695 m; **saloon inside headroom ~2.1 m at centre, ~1.6 m at the window head**. Interior white GRP panels follow the outer curve (tube-like vault).
- **Windows**: top follows the curved wall (arched top corner at the roof side - the window head is a quarter-round shape towards the centre, see P1790177 and 918). Window opening ~1.15-1.30 m wide x 0.95 m tall, sill at ~**1.00-1.05 m above floor** (seated eye height) and head at ~1.95 m above floor. Two windows per bay (bay = distance between doors ~ 4.8-5 m) separated by a ~0.55 m white pillar. Horizontal ventilation grille above the windows (long thin louvre ~0.12 m tall) and **LED strip lights above and along the window head**.
- Lower side wall: white panel with the seat backs; below the seat a **long white box (seat base) with dark grey/black front panel** (the "plinth" which hides heaters).

## 5. Doors
- **6 doors per side (12 per set)** (Wikipedia infobox). Photos show one wide door bay roughly every 6.5 m with a pair of large side windows between bays; use end cars **1 door per side** (about 4.3 m behind the cab front) and middle cars **2 doors per side**: 1+2+2+1 = 6 (distribution UNCERTAIN - inferred).
- Door opening ~**1.30 m wide x 1.85 m tall** (wide, double-leaf) with a rounded top where the door sits in the curved roof shoulder (the opening's top edge is an arch/curve following the body, not a rectangle). Two sliding **leaves with large arched-top windows** - each leaf's upper half is glazed (about 0.45 m wide x 1.1 m tall) and its lower half orange. Pocket sliding doors (slide into the body). 4-second delay between warning chime and opening; **white light strips beside doors flash during closing**.
- No protruding step; floor level with the platform (PSD-ready).
- Each door has a **dark grey wedge-shaped threshold and a yellow/black warning edge**; green emergency release button box inside beside the door (green with white symbol).

## 6. Interior (verified from Commons photos)
- **Open gangway** (full walk-through) the entire length of the 4 cars. At each articulation the gangway has a **black rubber bellows with a glossy black glass screen/gate-like portal** visible at the end car cab partition. Between cars there is **a soft arch frame in white with orange vertical hand poles at its sides**; the connecting floor is a **grey vinyl turn-plate**.
- **All seating is longitudinal** (side-facing, along the walls). busandtrainuser: each **middle car has one long 8-person bench plus a small 2-person section at the end; "total seating is 24"** (per middle car pair as written); end cars are longer and add a **wheelchair space with two tip-up seats** (P1790019: wheelchair pictogram on the black bench end panel). Other press quotes 118 seated / 204 standing for the whole train (~322 total, 310 officially). Model: benches of 8 and 2 and 3-seat sections in the end cars, plus **tip-up (flip) seats** (black/dark-grey frames, moquette cushions) mounted on the saloon wall beside the door vestibules (visible folded in Commons 919 and P1790013).
- Seat shape: a slim shell with **moquette seat and a high moulded back that is joined as one curved piece; black/dark-grey plastic base**; individual-seat contours are subtle; each bench has a **dark grey/black moulded end panel** with a see-through glass/Perspex draught screen at the door end.
- **Moquette**: charcoal/mid-grey ground (#4B4F57) with a pattern of **overlapping ring/circle motifs** in light lavender-grey (#A9A7C2 / #C7C7D6), darker grey, and **small orange rings** (#F28C1B) scattered. (Commons 2024 918.) It is a "new moquette"; the old trains had maroon/red check.
- **Floor**: dark grey speckled vinyl (#3A3C40) over most of the saloon, with a **lighter grey band (#8F9093) across the door thresholds** (colour-contrast flooring for the visually impaired).
- **Walls and ceiling**: white (#F4F4F2) smooth GRP panels, slightly glossy; the ceiling vault is white with **continuous linear LED strips** (cool white) running the full length in the centre of the crown AND a second pair of strips along the top of each window; a long **vent grille** (light grey louvre) sits above the windows. Discreet LED lighting (DCA).
- **Poles/handrails**: **bright orange** (#F26B1D) powder-coated vertical poles (~38 mm) in two rows along the saloon, each with a **curved top sweeping towards the middle** and fixed to a horizontal **orange overhead rail** ("roof-height grab rails"); orange stub **horizontal rails** on the wall pillars at hand height; the door area has a **pair of vertical orange poles on each side of the opening plus a curved orange pole in the centre of the vestibule** (two big orange arcs framing the door). Orange handrail on the sides of the tip-up seat areas.
- **Passenger information**: no in-car "next station" displays at launch (busandtrainuser); instead **illuminated screens on the end-wall panels show scrolling info and the network map**. Large **route diagram plaques on the white wall panel above the seats**: an oval/orange-ring loop (Outer / Inner circle) with station names around it (oval track-shaped diagram with an orange outline). Small circular roundel-stickers on the glass. **CCTV domes** at each end of each car (white-grey half-spheres in the roof corners and above the doors), an **SOS/passenger-help unit** (green button panel with grille, in a white box at each end of the saloon, ~1.3 m above floor), and two emergency contact points per coach.
- **Wheelchair/priority**: one wheelchair space (with pictogram and two tip-up seats) at the end of each end car; priority seating near doors on the longitudinal benches. A "flip-up" priority area is not separately marked.
- Advertising frames: planned; not seen in photos of the saloon walls.
- Air-conditioned (first on a deep-level UK underground train), per busandtrainuser.

## 7. Driver's cab
- **Hardly documented.** Known: during mixed-fleet and attended running the trains are driven from a **temporary cab behind a partition wall** (Rail Engineer), at the left of the front in the **front-end black glazing with a partition and sliding door** to the saloon; the cab is separated from the saloon by a **white partition with a door**, the rest of the front car being open to passengers. The cab front is blacked out for privacy (busandtrainuser). The partition and cab will be removed for UTO so that passengers can see out over the front.
- Depot movements: a **"flip-up control panel" at each end** lets staff drive in UTO mode (Rail Engineer).
- Modelling guidance (ESTIMATE, based on standard GoA2 driver desks): a compact **central desk (~0.8 m wide) with a tilting white/grey housing**, a **combined traction/brake master controller on the left**, **two or three 7-10 inch screens** (CBTC driver display, CCTV, diagnostics) on the desk top, door open/close buttons on the right (two green/red push buttons), a **ATO "start" button**, a horn pedal, a cab radio handset, and the central front door evacuation route in the middle. The driver sits slightly **left of the centre line**, seat dark grey, windscreen black tinted (privacy film on the outside).
- Control systems: Selectron (Stadler), ABB traction converters, CBTC by Hitachi (Ansaldo). GoA2 now, GoA4 (UTO) planned when signalling and PSDs complete.

## 8. Driverless-ready features
- Front gangway panel and cab are removable; open front view for passengers.
- CCTV in cars, controller-to-passenger intercom, door monitoring.
- Compatible with half-height (1.7 m) **platform screen doors** (Gilgen, 6 doors per side line up with the train doors): PSDs are white-framed with glass panels and **orange door leaves** at the leaf positions (Commons 20261021 Partick platform edge doors); installed at Partick, Govan and Ibrox by Sept 2026.

## 9. Uncertain items / to confirm
- Exact door bay distribution (1-2-2-1 inferred), exact window dimensions, exact cab desk layout.
- Orange tone differs between publicity renders and in-service units; the in-service units are paler white with orange door leaves only.
- Seat counts differ between sources (118 seated / 24 / etc.).
- The roundel's inner ring colour (white vs grey) varies between photographs; model as orange ring + white ring + grey disc with a white "S".

# Stations and tunnels - what they look like (for texturing)

Mostly read from Commons photos (cited by file name); the 2011-2020 SPT refit (about GBP 50 m, Hillhead first in 2011, all 15 done by 2020) replaced finishes with lower-cost materials, energy-efficient lighting, 28 new escalators, hearing loops, tactile paving and **colour-contrast flooring** (Rail Engineer, https://www.railengineer.co.uk/subway-revival-an-update-on-glasgows-modernisation/). Platform screen doors (half height, **1.7 m**, Gilgen) are being added: white frames, clear glass, **orange door leaves**, flush with the platform edge (Commons 20261021 Partick platform edge doors).

## Generic refit kit (use unless the table says otherwise)
- **Platform floor**: large-format **grey stone-effect tiles**, ~600 x 600 mm, mid grey (#8C8D8F to #9A9A9A, speckled), thin lighter joints; **yellow-ish/cream edge strip** (~0.1 m) along the platform edge, then a **~0.6 m dark grey (#555) tactile strip** (rows of studs, "hazard" dot pattern; lines of lozenge studs along the platform centre ~1 m from edge) - see St George's Cross and Kelvinbridge photos.
- **Wall name band**: continuous band at ~2.1-2.6 m above platform: **orange (#F05A1A) on the Outer-circle platform side, charcoal/navy-grey (#3B4048) on the Inner-circle side**, with the station name in white bold sans-serif (Kelvinbridge, St George's Cross, West Street, Govan). Direction signs: "Inner" (charcoal block) / "Outer" (orange block) with arrows (West Street hanging sign).
- **Line diagram panel** (Cessnock, West Street): white header with SPT roundel and bold navy word "Inner"/"Outer", black/dark-navy body listing next stops with orange squares joined by an orange line and minutes ("2 mins" ...), white text.
- **Roundel** SPT "S": orange ring, white ring, charcoal inner disc, white S.
- **Lighting**: surface-mounted white linear LED/fluorescent tubes along the vault crown and, in cut-and-cover/box stations, long **recessed LED strips** and round downlights in a **suspended white/silver metal tile ceiling** (600 x 600 / 300 x 1200 mm lay-in tiles).
- **Trackside**: concrete track trough with the **live (third) rail alongside, with a painted warning legend in yellow stencil "DANGER 4 LIVE RAIL 4"** along the cess wall (St Enoch/Partick/SGX). Dark, damp, grimy concrete and brick walls below platform level. Rail type/sleeper detail not verified (ESTIMATE: flat-bottom rail in a concrete trough, rusty brown).
- **Safety fence**: black powder-coated mesh fence between tracks at side-platform stations (St Enoch, Partick, Govan).

## Tunnel lining appearance (for the running tunnels)
- Nominal bore **3.35 m (11 ft)**; running tunnels vary by ground (Freyssinet case study https://www.freyssinet.co.uk/case-study/tunnel-refurbishments-glasgow-subway/): **brick circular**, **brick horseshoe**, **concrete horseshoe**, **brick-and-concrete circular**, and **cast-iron segmental** (under the rivers and large buildings, Rail Engineer). Lining condition: damp, water staining, lime/efflorescence streaks, drip sheets, resin-injected and sprayed-concrete patches, new weep pipes; cast-iron rings re-caulked.
- Colour/finish suggestions: **cast iron** = dark rust-brown/black (#2E2623) with raised flange ribs every ~0.6 m (ring width ~0.6 m, segments flanged, bolt heads); **brick** = soot-darkened red-brown (#4A3A33) in ring courses (header/stretcher), whitewash stains; **concrete** = dull mid-grey (#55575A) with form-marks; all uniformly sooty and wet. Tunnel mouths at stations show a **ring of dark brick headers (2-3 courses)** around the black bore (Commons St Enoch/Partick photos).
- Track (details ESTIMATED, not verified): flat-bottom rail, **third (live) rail on insulators** beside the running rails; 4 ft gauge; ballast-less concrete trough; yellow-stencilled warning "DANGER 4 LIVE RAIL 4" on the cess wall; cable runs and a cable tray along the tunnel wall; red/green signal lamps/ CBTC balises in the track centre; emergency walkway (cess) ~0.5 m wide beside the track.
- Station-end "hump": the platform is on a gentle summit of ~1 in 40 so tunnels fall away from both platform ends.
