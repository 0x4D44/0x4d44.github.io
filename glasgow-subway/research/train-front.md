Written by A1 for A3. Every statement below was read from photographs viewed with the Read tool; reference copies are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/52020da6-bb97-5dd3-88b8-ef2e78784739/scratchpad/ref_photos/` (NOT in the repo; licences/authors/URLs in `ref_photos/SOURCES.md`, summarised at the end). Scale: body width 2.309 m, height 2.642 m rail-to-roof (known); measurements in photos are +-0.05-0.1 m, perspective notes flagged. y = height above rail head, x = across (+x = train's right when looking forward), z = along (forward positive from the glass plane).

## 0. Headline corrections to the model
1. **The front is ONE huge black glazed panel, not a small windscreen.** It covers almost the whole front face: ~2.1 m wide x ~2.0 m tall; the white fibreglass is only a thin frame (0.18 m side margins, 0.08 m at the crown) plus a thin white "chin" below.
2. The glazing is divided into **three panes in a black frame: left pane (0.54 m), central evacuation DOOR (0.84 m, tall arched-top leaf), right pane (0.59 m with one wiper)**. In service the glass is blacked out so the divisions barely show.
3. **Two curved vertical LED "blades"** (crescents) sit inside the glass at left and right edges (warm white = headlights, RED = tail lights) each with a **2x4 cluster of small LEDs at its bottom end**. No round headlamps.
4. **Doors are DOUBLE (bi-parting): two leaves meeting on a vertical centre seam**, each leaf orange in its lower ~55%, glazed with a black-framed window in its upper part, and the whole opening has an orange surround. (Confirmed: gillfoto_921, rljh_2025-04-17, muns_InnoTrans_1.)
5. The roof is a smooth continuous arch (R~1.15 m) over the nose; no step. The roundel is on the **front lower-centre (illuminated orange "S" ring + word "Subway", underneath the door glass)** and on each side near the cab window.

- Front face in plan: central ~0.9 m nearly flat, then **corner radius ~0.6 m** wrapping into the sides; the black glass wraps ~0.15 m round the corners (visible in the 3/4 views as the black panel's edge curving).
- Side elevation: the glass face is **nearly vertical, leaning back ~5-7 degrees**; top of glass sits ~0.12 m behind the chin; roof line continues from the apex smoothly to the glass top with a radius ~0.6 m; no windscreen "peak".
- **Cab-side window** (single, behind the nose): rounded rectangle, black thick frame (0.05 m), ~0.57 m wide x 0.80 m tall, corners R~0.10 m, top edge slightly raked following the shoulder; **sill y~1.36, head y~2.16** (est. from spsmiler 303). Its front edge is ~0.6 m behind the front glass corner (white "cheek"), centre ~1.0 m behind the glass plane (ESTIMATE).
- Below the cab window: orange **"SPT"** logo (0.18 x 0.08 m) + white fleet number "303" (0.12 m tall) at y~1.0 m, and a **louvred vent grille 0.55 x 0.22 m** at y~0.75-0.97 (white/grey frame).
- Behind the window (0.2 m gap): **side roundel** (0.22 m dia., orange ring) at y~1.95 with the word "Subway" below it (y~1.65), then 0.5 m of plain white, then the first door.
- Lower body: white shell down to y~0.62, then **continuous black skirt band y 0.40..0.62** (underframe cover), small **orange rectangular hatch** (0.25 x 0.10 m) at skirt level near each car end (gillfoto_921/919).
- **Roof**: smooth, painted white at the sides with a darker/grey glossy **roof panel strip ~0.9 m wide** along the crown starting ~0.9 m behind the front edge (reflections in the 305 3/4-above photo); no pantograph (third rail), no visible roof boxes; vent/AC grilles are hidden in the cars' roofs.

## 3. DOORS (double, bi-parting)
Evidence: muns_InnoTrans_1 (door closed: orange frame, two leaves, vertical black seam), gillfoto_921 (door closed: two orange lower panels with a central seam, two tall windows), rljh_2025-04-17 (doors half open showing two leaves sliding apart), spsmiler_P1790021 (door fully open).
- **Type**: two-leaf bi-parting **sliding pocket doors** (leaves slide into the body skin on either side), flush with the body when closed; the opening has an **orange surround/jamb ~0.05 m wide that is part of the livery** (the orange "door zone": orange band ~0.55 m wide on each side of the opening running from the roof curve to the black skirt).
- **Opening**: width **~1.30 m** (two leaves of ~0.65 m), height from floor (0.695) to the curved top: **~1.80 m at the inboard edge, tapering because the top follows the roof shoulder**; the door head is an arch (top edge curved with R~1.1 m); outer face leaf height ~1.9 m.
- **Leaf**: lower ~55% (y 0.70..1.55) **solid orange (#F28C1B / #F26B1D)**, upper ~45% (y 1.55..2.30) a **window with black frame, tall rounded-top glass ~0.45 m wide x 0.70 m tall**, tinted, top corners rounded (R~0.2 m) following the arch. A thin black vertical seam (0.01 m) at the meeting edge; **no external handles**; a small round black emergency-release/lock plug at about 1.0 m height on the white body 0.35 m beside the opening (visible as a dot, "round black button" in 921 and 919).
- Beside the door on the white panel: yellow/black triangular warning decals (door trapping), a small grey sensor/plate; **door-side white LED light strip** inside the frame that flashes when closing (visible white vertical strip in the open door, P1790021).
- Interior door vestibule (for context): two orange poles per side of the opening, green emergency-release box ("green square with white symbol") beside it at ~1.2 m, black floor threshold with grey strip.
- **Count and position**: 12 doors per set = 6 per side. Photos show (muns_InnoTrans_1, foulger_047): the end car has **a door ~2.2-2.5 m behind the nose, a group of 3 windows, and a second door at the car's other end**; the semi-trailer cars have **one door each** => **2 + 1 + 1 + 2 per side** (best inference; strongly supported by the 4-body/6-door count and the two end-car doors visible in the 305 photo). Door centres from the front (ESTIMATE): 3.3 m; 9.9 m; 15.3 m; 24.0 m; 29.5 m; 36.0 m (the middle pair flank the windowless centre coupling at 19.6 m).
- Window pattern between doors (from 921 and 305): after each door: a pair or triple of windows each ~0.85 m wide x 0.75 m tall separated by 0.25 m white pillars; some bays have **one wide window ~1.8 m x 0.8 m**; the **centre (coupling) bay has NO windows** (Falk2 J33 699 description). All windows: black frames (0.04 m), rounded corners, top edge follows the shoulder, sills y~1.36.

## 4. Livery map (colours estimated from photos)
- Body: gloss **white** (#F4F3EE) all over, with **orange (#F26B1D)** only in the door zones and on small marks (SPT logo, roundel ring, handle, hatch).
- Black: front glazing and chin skirt, window surrounds, the continuous lower skirt band, roof strip (dark grey #3A3C40).
- Orange door zone shape: a vertical band ~1.7 m wide (2 x 0.55 m margins + 1.30 m opening) whose upper end **flares into the roof curve** (the orange follows the arch up to the crown at the InnoTrans train; on in-service units the orange is mostly the leaves + a narrow surround, with white between) - OBSERVE both: muns_InnoTrans_1 shows broad orange side bands; foulger_047/921 (in service) show the orange only on the leaves and a thin frame. Use the in-service version.
- Roundels: front lower centre (glass), side near cab; "Subway" in orange sans-serif.
- Numbers: fleet 301-317 in white under the glass / beside the cab window ("SPT 303").

## 5. Other relevant front-end details
- Auxiliary cab at one end only; both ends have the same exterior glazing (the exhibition train's back end shows the same door and panes).
- Through-view: at the exhibition train (no cab partition) you can see straight through the front glass the whole saloon: orange poles, ceiling LED strips, two dark-grey headrest-like seat backs near the floor centre (muns_InnoTrans_2) - in service the glass is blacked out for the driver's privacy.
- Underside: Jakobs-type bogies are open, black/grey with dark skirts; at the centre the shell-sleeve coupler with a black corrugated bellows (Falk2 707).
