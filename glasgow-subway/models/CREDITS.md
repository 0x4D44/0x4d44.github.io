## Attribution
People in `people.glb` / `people_lod.glb` were generated with MPFB2 (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender, GPL code used as a tool) from the CC0 MakeHuman base mesh, targets, skins, hair, eyes and clothes, plus CC-BY clothes by Elvaerwyn, Mindfront (Sweden), punkduck, MTKnife and culturalibre, all from the MakeHuman community asset repository (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets.html). CC-BY items: (c) the authors named below, licensed CC BY (3.0 / 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ ; textures were resized and re-compressed, meshes decimated and fitted. Tartan kilts, skirts, hose, sporran, sash, trews and all other models are our own work (generic setts, no official clan tartan is claimed).
