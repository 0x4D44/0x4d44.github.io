About this instrument

This began as a dare. A boy asked, mostly joking, for a website where you could watch paint dry — so we built one and took it seriously. The wall is a coarse film model: thousands of little patches of emulsion, each losing its wet sheen at a slightly different moment, so a drying front creeps across the surface the way it does on a real wall (edges and thin spots first). The clock is honest to the physics: a coat that is touch-dry in twenty minutes at 20 °C and 50% humidity will loiter for hours in a cold, damp room, and a full cure to a hardened film takes weeks. Warm it up, open a window, and watch the numbers fall.

Vanilla JavaScript on a single canvas. No frameworks, no build step, no paint fumes. The extractor-fan hum, if you switch it on, is synthesised. Best enjoyed slowly.
