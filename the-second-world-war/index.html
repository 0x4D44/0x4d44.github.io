About this atlas

The Second World War animates the war month by month from the annexations of 1938 to the surrender of Japan in 1945. Countries are tinted by who controlled them; bold lines trace the front where armies met; arrows mark the great offensives; markers flare at each battle; and the curve below charts the Battle of the Atlantic — the merchant tonnage sent to the bottom by the U-boats, month by month.

How to read it

  • Press play, or drag the time ribbon to scrub through the war.
  • Switch view between the European/Atlantic and Pacific theatres.
  • Hover a battle marker, a country or a ribbon tick for detail.

Sources & method

Dates, places and outcomes were assembled from standard references (Wikipedia, Britannica, official campaign histories) and put through a fact-checking pass. Front-line positions are interpolated between dated keyframes and are necessarily schematic.

Caveats

Country fills use modern borders — the only world atlas a static page can load — so the Soviet Union shows as its successor states, and the front-line overlay (not the fills) carries the true line of contact through the USSR. This is an illustration, not a substitute for a historical GIS.
