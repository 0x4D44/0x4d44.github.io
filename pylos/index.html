How to play

Goal. Each player has 15 spheres. Whoever places a sphere on the single top point — the apex — wins. If you cannot move and have no spheres, you lose.

Place. Drop a sphere from your reserve onto any empty spot on the base, or onto a raised spot once four spheres sit beneath it.

Raise. Instead of spending a sphere, lift one of your own free spheres up to a higher supported spot — as long as it isn't holding that spot up. Raising saves spheres.

Square. Complete a 2×2 square of your own colour and you may take back one — then optionally a second — of your free spheres.

Controls. Tap a glowing seat to place. Tap one of your free spheres to lift it, then tap a higher seat to raise it.
