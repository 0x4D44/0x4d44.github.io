    FIELD MANUAL

    How to survive the valley

    1

    Draw and resolve

    Turn over one Adventure card, read it, and carry it out if possible. You choose dinosaur routes, lava spaces and pteranodon mischief during your own turn.

    2

    Move one explorer

    Choose any one of your four surviving explorers. Land explorers roll one die. An explorer carrying treasure rolls two. River and swamp explorers move exactly one space without rolling.

    3

    Bring treasure home

    Enter the temple without exact count, take one coin, and escape by any of the four yellow arrows. Short game: first coin out. Long game: first three.

    7×

    Volcano

    Six single counters descend the cone. After that, each eruption adds three connected counters in the valley. Lava may cover any board space except the swamp and kills explorers beneath it.

    6×

    Pteranodon

    Move one of your explorers—even from outside, a lair, a lava cut-off or the swamp—to a safe empty space, or drop an opponent onto an empty dangerous space or one of the four separate inner-swamp spaces.

    19×

    Dinosaurs move

    Grazing, undergrowth, restless, danger and attack cards tell you exactly how many different dinosaurs move and how far. A dinosaur captures every explorer on every space it enters.

    6×

    Monster

    The swamp monster advances one grey arrow clockwise. Any explorer on its new space is eaten.

    7×

    Swamp

    Six cards place one expedition member on any grey-arrow space except the monster’s; explorers may share. They then move exactly one arrow clockwise per turn until leaving through either blue exit. The single Escape card jumps one of yours beyond the nearest exit.

    9×

    Water, fights & kit

    Water crises force a chosen explorer to reach a river on two dice or perish at the route’s end. Dinosaur fights free a captive one footprint outside a lair. Secret paths link caves and temple; guns unlock finite bullets at the two ammunition dumps.

    • No doubling back. An explorer may move in any direction but cannot enter the same space twice during one roll.
    • Water stops you. Wading from land into a river ends movement immediately. Starting in water—or climbing out—means one space only. Bridges are ordinary land.
    • Hazards block. Explorers cannot voluntarily enter the swamp or a lair, and cannot enter a space occupied by a dinosaur, the monster or lava.
    • Company is allowed. Any number of explorers may share a normal space or the temple.
    • Loose treasure needs exact count. Coins abandoned through capture, swamp or pteranodon can be claimed only when a movement roll ends exactly on them.
    • No exact count at the big doors. Entering the temple or leaving the valley ends movement immediately, whatever remains on the dice.
    • Dinosaurs obey the whole order. A chosen dinosaur must be able to complete the exact printed distance; unlike explorers, dinosaurs move freely and may retrace a space. Entering an explorer’s space interrupts the route for capture. A lair may be entered only as the final step.
    • Bullets are physical and finite. A gun card lets that expedition take one bullet when it lands on an ammunition dump. Each bullet frightens one dinosaur exactly one adjacent space, before or after normal explorer movement.
