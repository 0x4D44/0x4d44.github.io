    THE CASTLE HANDBOOK

    How to survive the night

    HOME

    Be first through either doorway.

    Each guest begins on the matching coloured START stone. On a red-and-yellow spin, Dracula prowls first; then the active guest moves exactly the yellow 3 or 4 stones. Paths may fork and double back between turns, but a stone cannot be used twice in one move.

    Exact movement

    Choose any highlighted destination. Entering HOME must be the final stone of the move.

    Human victory

    The first uncursed guest to reach HOME wins immediately. The Green Vampire must shed the mask before escaping.

    Share the path

    Guests may share stones and rooms. Plastic pieces do not block movement.

    Turn order

    Highest setup spin starts. The digital table resolves that opening spin automatically from the night number, then proceeds clockwise.

    64➜

    One pointer, two concentric instructions.

    The surviving Canadian spinner has eighteen equal outer sectors. Twelve are red: move Dracula 1–6 blood pools, then move the active guest 3 or 4 yellow stones as shown by the inner ring. Six coloured sectors align with the word VAMPIRE: move that plastic vampire straight to the numbered perch and do not move the active guest.

    DR

    Count Dracula

    He advances counter-clockwise on the separate blood trail. If he finishes in a room with an exposed human, one pawn is swallowed by the red cloak. The first victim becomes the Green Vampire; later victims are carried to the vault.

    GV

    The Green Vampire

    The cursed player wears the mask and moves on yellow stones on their own turn. Candle barriers are impassable. Catch an exposed guest in the same room and the mask passes; the released player dives into the nearest hidey-hole.

    BV

    The Blue Vampire

    This neutral bat flies only between perches 5 and 6. An exposed guest in its room is thrown back into the vault. This consequence is a labelled restoration because the surviving first instruction page does not preserve the blue bite paragraph.

    Yellow stones

    The movement network for human guests and the Green Vampire.

    Blood pools

    Dracula's independent thirty-pool circuit, beginning at the coffin.

    A guest ending here is invisible to every room-based bite.

    Humans cross normally. The Green Vampire cannot cross the marked doorway.

    Vampire perches

    Green uses 1–4; Blue uses 5–6. A coloured spinner result flies directly here.

    HOME
    Home doorways

    Either exit wins. They are deliberately reached by several competing routes.

    Restoration boundaries

    The spinner geometry, setup, Dracula/yellow movement, first-bite transformation, vault returns, hidey-holes, candle barriers, mask passing and engulfing pieces are directly evidenced. The precise Blue Vampire penalty, tie-breaking when several exposed guests share a room, the no-revisit movement clarification and the crypt's position on the board are stated openly as restoration decisions. See RESEARCH.md in the source for the evidence ledger.

    The evidence places the vault in the lower left and sends later victims to it, but does not fix how it connects. It is cut here as a dead-end stair off the vault antechamber: still a hidey-hole, still escapable, but a nine-stone climb back out that no single spin can shortcut — so being carried down costs a guest real time rather than handing them the shortest road to the doorway.

    All board, box, mask and piece artwork here is newly drawn SVG. No original scan or trademarked package illustration is shipped with the game.
