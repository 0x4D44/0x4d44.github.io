# Review 01 — hostile game historian

## Attack brief

Assume the page is an attractive modern invention wearing a 1977 name. Identify every place where it overclaims authenticity, copies commercial art, conflates editions, or silently fills a missing rule.

## Findings and changes

- **Critical:** the complete rulebook was unavailable. Added a three-level evidence ledger and made the Blue Vampire consequence explicitly restored in the rule modal, engine constants and research file.
- **High:** a probability-equivalent spinner would not be a physical restoration. Preserved the photographed clockwise sequence of all eighteen sectors.
- **High:** the first bite is the game's identity, not flavour text. Added the full Green Vampire role change, hollow-piece visual, mask cinematic, pass-the-curse phase and former-holder release.
- **High:** avoid scanned artwork. Rebuilt the box, castle, spinner, pawns, Dracula and both vampires as original inline SVG/CSS.
- **Medium:** catalogue records vary between 1977 and 1978. Presented 1977 as the UK release and noted 1978 Canadian sets rather than treating one as an error.
- **Medium:** the implementation's 60 stones, 30 pools and named rooms must not masquerade as verified component counts. Labelled them as digital geometry.
- **Medium:** do not quote long instruction text. Paraphrased the legible first page and included no scan.

## Verdict

Pass after documentation. The result is recognisably the same unusual game system and material culture, while uncertainty is visible rather than laundered into “official” rules.
