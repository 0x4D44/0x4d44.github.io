Accessibility

A bus anyone can board

Step-free, low-floor

The entire fleet is low-floor with a kneeling suspension and a ramp, so wheelchair users, buggies and the less mobile can board at street level. New double-deckers carry two wheelchair spaces.

Audio-visual announcements

“Next stop” is announced aloud and shown on screen across the fleet — vital for blind and partially-sighted passengers and anyone new to the city.

Quieter, clearer cabs

Electric and hybrid deckers cut noise and vibration, while camera-based mirror systems give drivers a wider, clearer field of view in poor weather.

Cleaner air

A 100% Euro6-or-better fleet since 2020 — and a growing electric core — directly improves air quality on Edinburgh’s busiest streets.
