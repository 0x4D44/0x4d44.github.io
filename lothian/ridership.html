What drives the numbers

Why Edinburgh keeps riding

The flat fare

A single adult fare anywhere on the city network since 2006 makes the bus the simplest way to move — no zones, no calculating, just tap and go.

The Ridacard

A season ticket loaded onto a smartcard, plus contactless “Tap Tap Cap” that automatically caps daily and weekly spend, keeps regular riders loyal.

Frequency

Trunk corridors run every few minutes from early morning to late night, with a full Night Bus network after midnight.

A compact, dense city

Edinburgh’s geography — tight, hilly, parking-scarce — plays to the strengths of a frequent, blanket bus network.
