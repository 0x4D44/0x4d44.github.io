Who owns Lothian

A municipal company

Four councils hold the shares; Edinburgh dominates with 91%. There are no private investors.

Arm’s length

Run as an independent commercial company since 1986 deregulation — the councils set strategy but don’t run day-to-day operations.

Profit recycled

Surpluses return to owners as dividends — £3.2m in 2024, the first since 2019 — or are reinvested in new, cleaner vehicles.

Electrification first

The 2025 dividend was suspended so the company could pour money into electric buses and depot charging instead.
