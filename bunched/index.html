A dispatcher's guide to a very Scottish phenomenon

Why do three buses come at once, after a half-hour wait?

You run a circular route round the Water of Leith. Six perfectly spaced buses. One pigeon. You will cause a bunch on purpose, then learn to cure it, using only a clipboard and the power to say "not yet".

About eight minutes to learn it properly. No pigeons were harmed. Several were chased.

    The fine print

    The Water of Leith Omnibus Company is fictional, which will come as a relief to everyone. The mechanism is not. It is the model from Newell and Potts, Maintaining a bus schedule (1964): passengers turn up at a steady rate, and a bus stays at each stop for a fixed minimum plus a few seconds per person who boards. Nothing in this game makes buses bunch. They manage it by themselves.
