How to play

Goal. Be the last player able to move. You win when your opponent can neither move a stack nor place a reserve.

Control. You may move any stack whose top piece is your colour — even if your piece sits atop the opponent's.

Move. Lift the top 1–5 pieces of a stack and slide them in a straight line — across or up/down, never diagonally — exactly as many squares as pieces you lifted. Jump freely over anything between.

Stack. Landing merges piles. No stack may stand taller than five: extra pieces spill off the bottom. Your own spilled pieces become reserves; the opponent's are captured for good.

Reserves. Instead of moving, drop one reserve onto any square — empty or not. They are powerful; spend them well.
