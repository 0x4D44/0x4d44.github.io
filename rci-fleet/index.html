Flagship

Icon of the Seas

The largest cruise ship ever built and the first in the fleet to run on liquefied natural gas. At 248,663 gross tons she carries up to 7,600 guests across 20 decks and eight neighbourhoods — crowned by the largest waterpark at sea.
